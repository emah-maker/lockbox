# Evidence: Competitive Analysis: Phone Box

## Summary
- **Job:** competitive-analysis
- **Subject:** Phone Box, a physical phone lockbox with touchscreen timer, servo latch, and press-count override.
- **Deliverable:** `docs/business-development/competitive-analysis-2026-07-19.md`
- Produced a source-backed competitive analysis of the phone-focus/lockbox market and a differentiation strategy for setting Phone Box apart.

## Work Completed
- Built a competitor universe across five groups: direct time-lock boxes (kSafe, generic Amazon boxes, Lockabox), adjacent physical-friction tools (Brick, Bloom, Blok, Unpluq, Foqos), substitutes (Opal and other app blockers, Yondr, GoAro, minimalist phones), and the do-nothing alternative.
- Researched each with primary product/pricing pages plus secondary reviews (NBC Select, Marie Claire, CBS News, buying guides), triangulated, with clickable citations and July 2026 access dates.
- Built a 9-dimension comparison matrix (positioning, enforcement/bypassability, true lockaway, timer/UX, override, price/model, distribution, weaknesses) with confidence flags.
- Defined a category frame ("smart focus lockbox"), four differentiation pillars, win/loss logic, and objection-handling talk tracks.
- Ranked threats and opportunities with mitigations; produced 30/90-day and 6-month recommendations plus follow-up FRAIM jobs.

## Key Decision
Do not compete as a cheap "phone jail." Position as a designed, programmable, opaque, one-time-purchase focus lockbox whose tunable override is a genuine safety feature. This is the whitespace between commodity dumb-dial boxes and expensive app-connected boxes/minimalist phones.
