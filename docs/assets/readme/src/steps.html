Step 01

Set the timer

Swipe hours, minutes and seconds on the touchscreen. Up to 9 hours.

Step 02

Drop the phone in

Close the opaque lid. No notifications leak through the case.

Step 03

It locks. For real.

A servo drives the latch shut and the countdown starts.

Step 04

Unlock when done

The latch releases at zero. Emergency? Press override 25×.
