# FRAIM Rule: application-identity

## Intent
Cloud-credit programs (AWS Activate, Google for Startups, Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub, NVIDIA Inception, etc.) reject applications when the applicant identity looks consumer rather than business. Get the identity right *before* filling forms — fixing it after rejection often takes weeks of back-and-forth.
