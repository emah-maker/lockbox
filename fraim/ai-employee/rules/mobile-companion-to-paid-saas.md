# FRAIM Rule: mobile-companion-to-paid-saas

## Intent
Define the architectural and policy pattern for shipping a mobile app that is a companion to an existing paid SaaS product (the "parent SaaS"). This pattern dictates the auth model, the IAP posture, the on-device storage rules, and the App Store / Play Store guideline citations to use when defending the design.
