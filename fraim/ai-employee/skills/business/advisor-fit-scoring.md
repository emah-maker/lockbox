# FRAIM Skill: advisor-fit-scoring

## Skill Input
- Interview notes for each advisor candidate (from the initial advisor interview/call).
- The startup's specific needs per competency category.
- Any program-defined ranking requirement (e.g., "rank-order, top pick per category") — read from applicable rules.

## Skill Output
- A per-candidate score on two dimensions, 1–5 each: **chemistry** (working-relationship fit) and **value** (ability to materially help the business).
- A rank-ordered shortlist per category with the top pick highlighted.
- A recommended **advisor test-project** per top candidate (a small, real, time-boxed task that reveals how they actually work before any equity commitment).
- A "replace" flag for any candidate scoring ≤2 on either dimension, with the next alternative to pursue.
