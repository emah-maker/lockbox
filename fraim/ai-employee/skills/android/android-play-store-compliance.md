# FRAIM Skill: android-play-store-compliance

## Skill Input
- The shipping AAB
- Permissions declared in `AndroidManifest.xml`
- Foreground service types declared
- Targeted regions (some require additional disclosures, e.g. Brazil's LGPD, India's DPDP)
- AI/ML feature usage (Play has its own AI disclosure expectations)

## Skill Output
- Manifest review: every permission has a justifiable use; sensitive permissions have on-device fallbacks where possible.
- Data Safety form complete (run via `google-play-data-safety-mapping`).
- Target SDK ≥ Play's current floor (`targetSdkVersion` 34 or later as of 2026).
- 64-bit requirement met (every AAB ships `arm64-v8a` and `x86_64` ABIs).
- AI feature disclosure where applicable.
- Content rating questionnaire completed and saved.
