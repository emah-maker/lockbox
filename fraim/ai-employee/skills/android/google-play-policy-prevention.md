# FRAIM Skill: google-play-policy-prevention

## Skill Input
- The shipping AAB and its `AndroidManifest.xml`
- The drafted Play Console store listing (short / full description, screenshots, feature graphic)
- The developer account history (number of apps, prior policy strikes)
- The Data Safety form draft and Privacy Policy URL
- Sensitive permissions declared (BACKGROUND_LOCATION, SMS, CONTACTS, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, etc.)

## Skill Output
A signed pre-submission checklist that documents how the binary, listing, and metadata address Play's most common policy rejection categories. Used as the input to App Content forms (so Play's reviewers see the justification up front) and as the basis for Policy Center appeals if a rejection is later cited.
