# FRAIM Skill: mvp-validation-design

## Skill Input
- The single hypothesis to test (problem, solution, willingness-to-pay, or usability — pick one).
- Current build state (idea only, mockup, concierge-capable, or working prototype).
- Available target customers to test with, and any program-required test quota (read from applicable rules; otherwise default to ≥2 customers).
- Resources/time available before the next decision point.

## Skill Output
- The chosen **validation vehicle** (mockup, concierge MVP, or interactive prototype) with the reason it fits the hypothesis and build state.
- A **test design**: who is tested, the exact task they perform, and the script/protocol for running it without leading them.
- The **kill/confirm signal**: the specific, pre-committed observation that would confirm the hypothesis vs. the one that would kill or pivot it — decided before the test runs.
- A **feedback-capture protocol** and a decide step (continue / iterate / pivot) tied to the signal.
