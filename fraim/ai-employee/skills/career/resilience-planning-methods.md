# FRAIM Skill: resilience-planning-methods

## Skill Input
- The person's current role and stressors (workload, uncertainty, financial pressure, isolation) — applies to any professional or founder.
- Existing coping tactics and support system (who they turn to).
- Any program-defined application profile (e.g., a founder "emotional rollercoaster" framing or a partner-support check) — read from applicable rules.

## Skill Output
- A **whole-life stressor inventory** (work, relationships, finances, health) ranked by impact and controllability.
- A short, concrete **coping-tactic plan** matched to the top stressors (evidence-based, sustainable, specific — not "exercise more").
- An **identity map**: a set of meaningful identities beyond the primary role, so self-worth is not tied to one thing (e.g. a founder whose only identity is "founder of X" feels like a failure if X fails).
- A **support-system map**: named people/resources for different needs (decisions, venting, expertise, crisis) and gaps to fill.
- A **key-relationship alignment plan**: a structured conversation guide for aligning with a partner/family/co-founder on the demands ahead, including how to surface and act on their level of support.
