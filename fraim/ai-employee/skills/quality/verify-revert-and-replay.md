# FRAIM Skill: verify-revert-and-replay

## Skill Input
- `verificationClaim`: The fix or change that needs proof.
- `availableProofSurface`: Existing repro steps, failing test target, or observable behavior that can prove the claim.
- `constraints`: Safety or repo constraints that might make revert-based proof unsafe.

## Skill Output
- `verificationPatternDecision`: Whether to use `revert-and-replay`, reject it, or choose a fallback pattern.
- `verificationBrief`: Exact instructions for the verifier, including what to run, what should fail, what should pass, and what evidence to capture.
- `evidenceRubric`: The minimum evidence the manager should require before accepting the claim.
