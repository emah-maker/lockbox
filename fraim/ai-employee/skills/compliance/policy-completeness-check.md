# FRAIM Skill: policy-completeness-check

## Skill Input
- `activeRegulations`: Array of regulation codes the run covers. Supplied by the caller.
- `policiesDir`: Path to the project's policy directory (typically `docs/compliance/policies/`). Supplied by the caller.
- `policyTemplateSlugs`: List of policy slugs available in the template library (caller fetches templates via `get_fraim_file` as needed).

## Skill Output
- `missingPolicies`: Policies that are required by at least one active regulation but are not present in `docs/compliance/policies/`.
- `stalePolicies`: Policies whose frontmatter `last-reviewed` is older than 12 months.
- `incompleteFrontmatter`: Policies missing required frontmatter keys (`owner`, `last-reviewed`, `related-controls`, `applicable-regulations`).
