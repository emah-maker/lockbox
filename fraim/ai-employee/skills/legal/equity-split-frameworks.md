# FRAIM Skill: equity-split-frameworks

## Skill Input
- Founding team: each member's role, time commitment (full/part-time), cash invested, prior IP/assets contributed, and opportunity cost.
- Whether the team wants a fixed split or a dynamic/contribution-based split.
- Any program eligibility constraints (e.g., entity type) — read from applicable rules.

## Skill Output
- A recommended equity split with the reasoning per founder, expressed as percentages that sum to 100%.
- The chosen **framework** (equal split, weighted-factor split, or dynamic/slicing-pie) with why it fits this team.
- A **vesting recommendation**: schedule (default 4-year), cliff (default 1-year), and acceleration terms to discuss.
- Open items requiring legal counsel and a handoff to `cap-table-modeling` for the ledger.
