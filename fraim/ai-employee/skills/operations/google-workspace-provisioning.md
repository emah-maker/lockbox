# FRAIM Skill: google-workspace-provisioning

## Skill Input
- `primaryDomain`: Registered company domain.
- `companyName`: Legal company name for billing and admin console.
- `tierChoice`: Approved tier (`Business Starter` | `Standard` | `Plus` | `Enterprise`).
- `billingEmail`: Billing contact on the company domain.
- `initialUsers`: Array of `{ primaryEmail, firstName, lastName, role }` for founding team.
- `superAdmins`: At least two primary emails to be granted super-admin role.
- `groupsAndAliases`: Groups (e.g. `team@{domain}`) and aliases (e.g. `hello@`, `support@`, `billing@`) to create.
- `sharedDrivePlan`: Shared drive name and folder structure.
- `dnsAccess`: `{ provider, canEditViaApi }`.
- `existingMailState`: Current MX routing state and approved cutover window.

- `adminBaselineState`: Status of each enforced setting.

- `mxCutoverState`: `pending` / `completed` / `deferred` with post-cutover delivery test result.
