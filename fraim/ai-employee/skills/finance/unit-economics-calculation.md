# FRAIM Skill: unit-economics-calculation

## Skill Input
- CAC data: total sales and marketing spend by period, and new customer count by period. Minimum: 6 months of data.
- LTV inputs: average contract value or ARPU, gross margin %, average customer lifespan or churn rate.
- Cohort data (optional): retention curves or revenue-per-cohort data to compute LTV from observed behavior rather than formula assumptions.
- Business model: SaaS subscription, transactional, marketplace, services, or other (affects which metrics apply).

## Skill Output
- **CAC**: Blended CAC (total S&M ÷ new customers). Payback period (months to recover CAC from gross margin contribution).
- **LTV**: Formula-based LTV (ARPU × Gross Margin % ÷ Churn Rate) and, if cohort data is provided, cohort-observed LTV at 12, 24, and 36 months.
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: The primary unit economics health metric. Benchmarks: > 3× = healthy, 1–3× = marginal, < 1× = structurally unprofitable at current economics.
- **Magic Number**: (Net new ARR in period × Gross Margin %) ÷ Prior period S&M spend. Benchmarks: > 0.75 = efficient, 0.5–0.75 = acceptable, < 0.5 = inefficient S&M spend.
- **Payback Period**: Months to recover blended CAC from gross margin contribution per customer. Target: < 18 months for SaaS.
- **Unit Economics Narrative**: One-paragraph interpretation of the metrics with specific improvement levers.
