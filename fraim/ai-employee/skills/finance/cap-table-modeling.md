# FRAIM Skill: cap-table-modeling

## Skill Input
- Founder & co-founder list with agreed (or proposed) ownership percentages or share counts.
- Advisors, employees, and any others promised equity, with amounts and vehicle (shares, options, %).
- Instruments outstanding: SAFEs, convertible notes, priced rounds (amount, valuation cap, discount, pre/post-money).
- Option pool target (% post-financing), if any.
- Any program-required line items (e.g., investor warrants) — read from applicable program rules; never assume.

## Skill Output
- **Fully-diluted cap table**: every holder, share class, share count, and ownership % that sums to 100%.
- **Option pool**: reserved, granted, and available, with the post-pool dilution shown.
- **Scenario lines**: pre-money vs post-money ownership for each outstanding instrument converted at the modeled round.
- **Dilution view**: founder ownership before and after the modeled financing + pool top-up.
- **Consistency check result**: percentages reconcile to 100%, no negative or unallocated shares, SAFE/note conversions math-checked.
