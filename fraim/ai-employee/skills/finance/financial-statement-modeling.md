# FRAIM Skill: financial-statement-modeling

## Skill Input
- Revenue data: historical actuals (monthly or quarterly) for the last 12–24 months. Source: P&L, accounting system export, or manually provided table.
- Cost data: COGS breakdown, OpEx by category (S&M, R&D, G&A), and any known one-time items.
- Balance sheet snapshot: cash, receivables, payables, debt schedule, and equity (at least one period end).
- Driver assumptions: growth rate, gross margin targets, headcount plan, or key contract terms that govern future revenue.
- Forecast horizon: 12 months (operating model) or 36 months (investor model).

## Skill Output
- **Income Statement**: Revenue → Gross Profit → EBITDA → Net Income. Monthly for 12-month models; quarterly for 36-month models. Actuals in one column band, forecast in a second.
- **Cash Flow Statement**: Direct or indirect method. Operating cash flow, CapEx, financing activities, ending cash balance.
- **Balance Sheet**: Assets, liabilities, and equity reconciling to zero. Ending cash ties to cash flow statement.
- **Key Metrics Summary**: ARR (if applicable), Gross Margin %, EBITDA Margin %, Burn Rate (monthly net cash out), Runway (months of cash at current burn).
- **Model Assumptions Log**: Every forecast assumption documented with source (historical average, management guidance, or benchmark).
