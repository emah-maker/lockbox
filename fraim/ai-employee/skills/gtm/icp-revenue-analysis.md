# FRAIM Skill: icp-revenue-analysis

## Skill Input
- CRM deal records: closed-won and closed-lost deals with attributes including company size, industry, funding stage, buyer title, deal stage at loss, deal source, ACV, close date, sales cycle days, and lost reason (CSV, structured text, or API-pulled data).
- Sales call transcripts (optional): raw text or structured summaries from any call recording tool.
- Transaction/subscription records (optional): customer tenure, expansion events, renewal outcomes, and churn events.
- Analysis scope: `acquisition` (Bowtie left bow only), `expansion` (Bowtie right bow only), or `full` (both bows).
- ICP cluster count target: 2–3 clusters.

## Skill Output
- ICP cluster definitions: 2–3 clusters each with company attributes, deal count, ACV (P25/P50/P75), close rate, average sales cycle days, and win rate vs. named competitors.
- Bowtie left-bow conversion rates (acquisition scope): stage-by-stage conversion from Educate→Engage→Validate→Sign, with the single highest-dollar drop-off stage identified.
- Bowtie right-bow health metrics (expansion scope): time-to-first-adopt, adoption milestone hit rate, expansion trigger hit/miss rate, renewal rate per ICP cluster, and advocate activation rate.
- Lost-reason distribution (when acquisition data available): categorized by price, competition, timing, and fit — weighted by ACV.
- Objection patterns (when call transcripts provided): ≥3 recurring patterns with the SPICED dimension most often missing at deal loss.
- Data quality flags: fields with <80% completion that limit analysis confidence, with an explicit impact statement per flag.
