# FRAIM Skill: outbound-sequence-design

## Skill Input
- ICP definition: 1–3 ideal customer profiles with company attributes (size, industry, tech stack), buyer title, and the primary pain this ICP experiences.
- Value proposition: the specific outcome the product delivers for each ICP. Must be ICP-specific — not a generic tagline.
- Sequence goal: the call-to-action the sequence is designed to produce (demo booked, intro call, content download, event registration).
- Social proof available: customer names (or anonymized logos), case study results, or user counts the rep can reference.
- Sequence channel availability: which channels the team can operate (cold email, LinkedIn, cold call, SMS).

## Skill Output
- **Multi-touch Outbound Sequence**: ≥ 5 touches across ≥ 2 channels. Each touch specifies: day offset, channel, subject line or opening line, body (≤ 150 words), and CTA.
- **Personalization Layer**: For each ICP, the specific attributes that must be customized per prospect: company name, industry-specific pain reference, trigger event (if available), or role-specific outcome.
- **Channel Cadence**: The day-by-day sequence map showing which channel fires on which day and the total sequence duration.
- **Break-up Sequence**: The final 1–2 touches explicitly framed as a last attempt — politely direct, with a soft opt-out offer.
