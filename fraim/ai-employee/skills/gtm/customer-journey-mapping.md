# FRAIM Skill: customer-journey-mapping

## Skill Input
- ICP profile list: 1–3 ICP clusters, each with company attributes and buyer title.
- Motion type per ICP: PLG, sales-assisted PLG, or outbound enterprise.
- Existing journey artifacts (optional): any `docs/gtm/gtm-system-design-*.md` or equivalent documents describing current stage definitions.

## Skill Output
- Bowtie journey map per ICP cluster: all 9 stages (Educate, Engage, Validate, Sign, Onboard, Adopt, Expand, Renew, Advocate) with, for each stage:
  - Entry criterion: the observable signal that a contact or account has entered this stage.
  - Exit criterion: the observable signal that justifies progression to the next stage.
  - Buyer action: what the buyer does in this stage.
  - Seller/CS action: what the seller (left bow) or CS/growth team (right bow) does in this stage.
  - Primary failure mode: the most common reason an account stalls or exits here without progressing.
- Sign stage handoff definition: explicit Sales→CS handoff checklist (SPICED dimensions confirmed, success criteria agreed, onboarding date set).
- Motion-type annotations: per-stage notes where PLG vs. sales-assisted PLG vs. outbound enterprise materially changes the buyer or seller action.
