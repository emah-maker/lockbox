# FRAIM Skill: ppc-account-structure

## Skill Input
- Campaign goals: the primary KPI (conversions, leads, ROAS, awareness) and secondary KPIs.
- Product or service catalog: the distinct offerings that need separate campaign targeting.
- Geographic targeting: countries, regions, or cities to target; any exclusions.
- Audience data: existing customer lists, CRM segments, or remarketing audiences available.
- Budget allocation: total monthly ad spend and any pre-assigned allocation by product or channel.
- Existing account structure (if present): current campaign/ad group hierarchy for audit purposes.

## Skill Output
- **Campaign Hierarchy**: Account → Campaign → Ad Group → Ad and Keyword level. Each level with naming convention, settings, and rationale.
- **Campaign Settings Specification**: For each campaign type (Search, Display, Performance Max, Demand Gen): bid strategy, network settings, audience signals, and budget allocation.
- **Ad Group Structure**: Ad group segmentation logic — by product, intent tier (branded/non-branded/competitor), or audience segment. Ad group keyword count (target: 10–20 tightly themed keywords per ad group).
- **Naming Convention**: Consistent naming for campaigns and ad groups using the format: `[Platform]-[Product]-[Match Type/Audience]-[Geo]` (e.g., `GGL-ProductA-Branded-US`).
- **Quality Score Optimization Plan**: For each ad group, the expected keyword-to-ad relevance gaps and the specific changes needed to achieve Quality Score ≥ 7.
