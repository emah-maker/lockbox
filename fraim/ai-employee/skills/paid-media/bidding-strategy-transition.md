# FRAIM Skill: bidding-strategy-transition

## Skill Input
- Current bid strategy: Manual CPC, Enhanced CPC, Target CPA, Target ROAS, Maximize Conversions, or Maximize Conversion Value.
- Conversion data: 30-day conversion count per campaign, conversion value (if applicable), and cost-per-conversion history.
- Performance goal: the target CPA, target ROAS, or conversion volume goal for the campaign.
- Budget constraints: minimum and maximum acceptable daily budget fluctuation during the transition period.
- Campaign history: account age, quality score distribution, and any prior bid strategy transitions.

## Skill Output
- **Transition Recommendation**: The target bid strategy, the rationale, and the prerequisite data thresholds that must be met before transitioning.
- **Transition Plan**: Step-by-step timeline for the transition with observation windows and rollback triggers.
- **Learning Period Protocol**: The specific constraints to apply during the smart bidding learning period (no budget changes, no structural changes, no bid adjustments).
- **Performance Benchmarks**: The pre-transition baseline metrics and the success thresholds that confirm the new strategy is performing.
