# FRAIM Skill: job-ownership-assignment

## Skill Input
- `jobDraft`: the new or changed job, including its domain, intent, and steps.
- `changeMode`: `teach` (net-new job) or `customize` (improving an existing job).
- `existingOwner` (when `changeMode` is `customize`): the employee that already owns the job.
- `hiredEmployees`: the manager's current team, each with a role/domain and identity.

## Skill Output
- `recommendedOwner`: the employee that should own the job.
- `ownerRationale`: a short, manager-readable reason for the recommendation.
- `newSpecialistProposal` (only when justified): a proposed new specialist employee, including its role, when no existing employee fits.
- `requiresManagerApproval`: `true` whenever the plan proposes creating a new specialist.
