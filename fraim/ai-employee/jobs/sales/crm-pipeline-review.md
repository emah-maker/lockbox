# FRAIM Job: crm-pipeline-review

## Intent
Keep the revenue pipeline moving — surface stalled deals, forecast pipeline health by stage and owner, identify net-new demand signals, and draft re-engagement proposals for high-value at-risk opportunities.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable:** A .pptx presentation at docs/sales/crm-pipeline-review-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing pipeline health dashboard with coverage ratio and stage velocity metrics, ranked stalled deal list with re-engagement recommendations, new demand signal briefs, and draft re-engagement proposals
- Pipeline health dashboard: coverage ratio, stage velocity, close date risk
- Ranked stalled deal list with re-engagement recommendations
- New demand signal briefs: recently-acquired accounts and target-market opportunities without open Opportunities
- Draft re-engagement proposals written as Tasks on top-5 stalled high-value deals
- CRM Opportunity records updated with stall reason and AI-recommended next step

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `connect`
2. Phase 2: `load-pipeline`
3. Phase 3: `analyze-pipeline-health`
4. Phase 4: `identify-stalled-deals`
5. Phase 5: `scan-demand-signals`
6. Phase 6: `draft-proposals`
7. Phase 7: `update-crm-records`
8. Phase 8: `produce-summary`
9. Phase 9: `submit`
