# FRAIM Job: cross-functional-dependency-management

## Intent
Surface, map, and actively manage cross-functional dependencies — so the delivery team is not surprised by blockers that were visible weeks in advance but never escalated.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/delivery-ops/cross-functional-dependency-management-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx containing the Dependency Map, Blocker Resolution Log, and Handoff Coordination matrix with owner assignments and risk classifications.
- **Dependency Map**: All cross-functional dependencies with owner, ETA, and risk classification.
- **Blocker Resolution Log**: Active blockers with resolution status and escalation history.
- **Handoff Coordination**: Agreed handoff criteria and timing for cross-team deliverables.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `dependency-discovery`
2. Phase 2: `owner-mapping`
3. Phase 3: `blocker-resolution`
4. Phase 4: `handoff-coordination`
5. Phase 5: `dependency-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
