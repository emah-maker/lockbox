# FRAIM Job: project-plan-creation

## Intent
Build a project plan with clear scope, dependency map, milestone sequence, and risk register — so the team knows what's being built, in what order, who owns each piece, and what could derail the schedule.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/delivery-ops/project-plan-{project-name}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx containing the scope definition (WBS with in/out items), dependency map with owners, milestone sequence with target dates and success criteria, and risk register with probability/impact scoring.
- **Scope Definition**: Work breakdown structure with clearly in-scope and out-of-scope items.
- **Dependency Map**: Cross-team and technical dependencies identified with owners.
- **Milestone Sequence**: Key milestones with target dates, success criteria, and owners.
- **Risk Register**: Top risks with probability, impact, and mitigation.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `scope-intake`
2. Phase 2: `dependency-mapping`
3. Phase 3: `milestone-sequencing`
4. Phase 4: `risk-register`
5. Phase 5: `project-plan-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
