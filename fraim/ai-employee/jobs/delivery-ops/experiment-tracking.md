# FRAIM Job: experiment-tracking

## Intent
Track an experiment from hypothesis intake through outcome measurement to a documented go/no-go decision — so experiments produce learnings that inform decisions, not just results that get ignored.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/delivery-ops/experiment-tracking-{experiment-name}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx containing the hypothesis statement, treatment/control definitions, outcome tracking log, statistical significance analysis, and go/no-go decision with rationale.
- **Hypothesis and Success Criteria**: Documented before the experiment starts.
- **Outcome Tracking**: Results tracked at the correct cadence with statistical rigor noted.
- **Go/No-Go Decision**: A clear, documented decision with rationale.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `experiment-intake`
2. Phase 2: `outcome-tracking`
3. Phase 3: `go-no-go-decision`
4. Phase 4: `experiment-submission`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
