# FRAIM Job: tax-strategy-planning

## Intent
Provide a structured tax planning framework that identifies the entity structure, available deductions, and quarterly estimate obligations — to ensure founders make informed tax decisions before deadlines, not after.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/finance/tax-strategy-planning-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with entity structure analysis, deduction inventory with IRC basis and documentation requirements, quarterly estimated tax payment schedule with due dates, CPA handoff checklist, and required documentation list.
- **Entity Analysis**: Assessment of current entity type and any restructuring considerations.
- **Deduction Inventory**: Documented list of applicable deductions with supporting rationale.
- **Quarterly Estimates**: Calculated estimated tax payments for upcoming quarters.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `entity-structure-review`
2. Phase 2: `deduction-inventory`
3. Phase 3: `quarterly-estimate-calc`
4. Phase 4: `planning-doc`
5. Phase 5: `tax-strategy-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
