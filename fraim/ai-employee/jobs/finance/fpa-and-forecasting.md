# FRAIM Job: fpa-and-forecasting

## Intent
Build a driver-based financial forecast and annual operating plan that replaces static budget spreadsheets with a living model that updates when business assumptions change.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/finance/fpa-and-forecasting-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing driver-based annual operating plan with 4-8 identified drivers, base/bull/bear scenario analysis, department-level budget tables, headcount plan, revenue targets, and board summary.
- **Driver Model**: 4–8 key business drivers identified and parameterized.
- **Three Scenarios**: Base, bull, and bear cases with explicit assumption sets.
- **AOP Summary**: Department-level budget with headcount, OpEx, and revenue targets.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `driver-identification`
2. Phase 2: `model-build`
3. Phase 3: `scenario-analysis`
4. Phase 4: `aop-review`
5. Phase 5: `fpa-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
