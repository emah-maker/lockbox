# FRAIM Job: financial-analysis

## Intent
Build a linked three-statement financial model that gives founders and CFOs a single source of truth for revenue, burn, and cash position — with scenario modeling and board-ready output.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/finance/financial-analysis-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing linked three-statement model with P&L, balance sheet, cash flow statements, ratio analysis dashboard (gross margin, burn multiple, CAC payback, LTV:CAC), and base/bull/bear scenario modeling.
- **Ratio Analysis**: Gross margin, burn multiple, CAC payback, and LTV:CAC calculated and trended.
- **Scenario Modeling**: Base, bull, and bear scenarios with key driver sensitivities.
- **Board Export**: Executive summary with burn runway and inflection points highlighted.
