# FRAIM Job: monthly-close-review

## Intent
Run the monthly financial close cycle — collect actuals, compute budget variances, write management commentary, and distribute the board package — in 2 hours instead of a full day.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/finance/monthly-close-review-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing budget variance analysis table, top 5 material variances with root cause commentary, summary KPIs (gross margin, EBITDA actual vs budget), and executive summary with cash runway.
- **Variance Analysis**: Every major P&L line analyzed with favorable/unfavorable/material classification.
- **Management Commentary**: Top 5 variances explained with root cause and forward-looking implication.
- **Board Package**: Executive summary formatted for board distribution.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `actuals-collection`
2. Phase 2: `variance-analysis`
3. Phase 3: `commentary-authoring`
4. Phase 4: `distribution`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
