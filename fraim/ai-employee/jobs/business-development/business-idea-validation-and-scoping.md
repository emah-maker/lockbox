# FRAIM Job: business-idea-validation-and-scoping

## Intent
To help founders validate business ideas and scope opportunities by transitioning from a "vague spark" to an "evidence-backed validation report" with concrete feature deliverables. This job combines hypothesis testing, market research, and strategic scoping to answer: *"Should we build this?"* and *"What should we build first?"*

This job focuses on internal decision-making and product validation. For investor presentations and fundraising, use the `investor-pitch-preparation` job which builds upon these validation findings.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: a .docx review copy generated from the final artifact via the author-docx skill at submission; this is the outcome delivered for review, with the .md below as the canonical agent-side record.
- Final artifact: A focused validation and scoping report at `docs/business-development/business-validation-report-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`. Each phase writes its detailed analysis as a section of this one report as the job progresses; do not create separate per-phase detail files.
- **Validated Hypotheses**: Evidence-backed value propositions and market assumptions
- **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**: Defined target customer characteristics and micro-segments
- **Market Evidence**: Research findings on problem intensity, demand signals, and competitive landscape
- **Basic Market Sizing**: Lightweight TAM/SAM/SOM analysis for internal planning
- **Prioritized Features**: A repo-ready backlog of features aligned with validated customer needs
- **MVP Scope**: Clear next steps for development or pivot recommendation

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `hypothesis-definition`
2. Phase 2: `deep-context-synthesis`
3. Phase 3: `socratic-assumption-challenging`
4. Phase 4: `market-demand-validation`

6. Phase 6: `market-research-and-stress-testing`
7. Phase 7: `feature-synthesis`
8. Phase 8: `mvp-scope-definition`
9. Phase 9: `validation-documentation`
10. Phase 10: `scoping-submission`
11. Phase 11: `address-feedback`
