# FRAIM Job: problem-statement-crystallization

## Intent
Transform vague product concepts or solution ideas into testable problem statements and narrow target market definitions that can drive effective customer development and product strategy.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: a .docx review copy generated from the final artifact via the author-docx skill at submission; this is the outcome delivered for review, with the .md below as the canonical agent-side record.
- Final artifact: A problem clarity document at `docs/business-development/problem-statement-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`
- **Testable Problem Statement**: One sentence describing specific friction or failure in current workflows
- **Narrow Target Market**: Behavioral and contextual specifics, not just demographics
- **Validation Hypothesis**: Falsifiable claim about the problem's frequency and impact
- **Success Metrics**: How to measure if the problem is real and worth solving
- **Current Alternatives**: What people do today and why it fails

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `concept-analysis`
2. Phase 2: `socratic-refinement`
3. Phase 3: `statement-synthesis`
4. Phase 4: `validation-framework`
5. Phase 5: `documentation`
6. Phase 6: `submission`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
