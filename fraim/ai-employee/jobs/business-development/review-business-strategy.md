# FRAIM Job: review-business-strategy

## Intent
Evaluate the quality of a founder's business strategy artifacts — business plan, pricing strategy, competitive analysis, and founder-market fit — regardless of how they were created. This job does not create artifacts; it reviews existing ones and produces a quality score for the founder journey quality dashboard.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: a .pptx synthesis deck generated from the review artifact at submission; this is the outcome delivered for review, with the .md below as the canonical agent-side record.
- Final artifact: `docs/business-development/business-strategy-quality-review.md`
- **Quality Scores**: Flat sub-scores (0-10) for market evidence, competitive rigor, unit economics, and strategic coherence
- **Composite Score**: Overall business strategy quality (0-10), emitted to `fraim_quality_scores`
- **Coaching**: Specific, actionable recommendations for the highest-impact improvements
- **Gap Analysis**: What's missing or weak across the strategy artifacts

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `artifact-discovery`
2. Phase 2: `business-plan-review`
3. Phase 3: `pricing-strategy-review`
4. Phase 4: `competitive-positioning-review`
5. Phase 5: `cross-artifact-coherence`
6. Phase 6: `quality-score-emission`
7. Phase 7: `review-submission`
