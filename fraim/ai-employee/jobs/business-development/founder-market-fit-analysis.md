# FRAIM Job: founder-market-fit-analysis

## Intent
To assess founder-market fit by analyzing the founder's background, expertise, and LinkedIn network against specific target market requirements. This job focuses on the founder's credibility and connections rather than general market analysis, identifying specific people from their network who could serve as co-founders, advisors, or warm introduction sources.

## Outcome
The founder walks away knowing exactly what to do next and who to contact. Specifically:

- **Whether they are the right person to build this**: a credibility score with specific evidence for each strength and gap, relative to what target-market buyers and investors expect.
- **Who to contact this week**: named individuals from their existing network - connection degree, mutual count, and a ready-to-send outreach message for each. No cold research needed.
- **What each person fills**: each candidate mapped to the specific credibility or GTM gap they address, so the founder can prioritize outreach by the gap that matters most.
- **What to do in the next 90 days**: a ranked Gap Priority Matrix with effort estimates, so the highest-leverage actions are immediately obvious without reading the full report.
- **The supporting case**: credibility assessment, experience gap analysis, and network map backing the recommendations - available for due-diligence conversations with investors, advisors, or partners.

Deliverables: `docs/business-development/founder-market-fit-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` (canonical) and a `.docx` review copy with Track Changes enabled.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `founder-background-assessment`
2. Phase 2: `linkedin-network-analysis`
3. Phase 3: `market-expertise-evaluation`
4. Phase 4: `gap-analysis-and-recommendations`
5. Phase 5: `co-founder-matching`
6. Phase 6: `fit-documentation`
7. Phase 7: `fit-submission`
8. Phase 8: `address-feedback`
9. Phase 9: `retrospective`
