# FRAIM Job: competitive-analysis

## Intent
Create a focused, source-backed competitive analysis for a company, product, feature, market category, or strategic question. This job answers: "Who are we really competing against, how do we compare, where are we exposed, and how should we position or respond?"

Use this job when the requested output is a standalone competitive analysis. Use `business-plan-creation` when competitive analysis is only one section of a broader business plan. Use `review-business-strategy` when the user already has a competitive analysis and wants its quality reviewed.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: a .docx review copy generated from the final artifact via the author-docx skill at submission; this is the outcome delivered for review, with the .md below as the canonical agent-side record.
- Final artifact: A focused competitive analysis at `docs/business-development/competitive-analysis-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`.
- **Competitor Set**: Direct competitors, adjacent competitors, substitutes, manual "do nothing" alternatives, and likely future entrants.
- **Comparison Matrix**: Source-backed matrix across positioning, buyer, product surface, pricing, distribution, proof points, moat, weaknesses, and likely objections.
- **Differentiation Strategy**: Clear positioning pillars, sales talk tracks, and defensible "why we win / why we lose" logic.
- **Threat Assessment**: Strategic risks, copycat exposure, distribution disadvantages, and opportunities to exploit competitor gaps.
- **Actionable Recommendations**: Product, marketing, sales, and research next steps.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `context-gathering`
2. Phase 2: `competitor-discovery`
3. Phase 3: `source-backed-market-research`
4. Phase 4: `competitive-matrix-creation`
5. Phase 5: `positioning-and-differentiation`
6. Phase 6: `threat-and-opportunity-assessment`
7. Phase 7: `strategic-recommendations`
8. Phase 8: `artifact-assembly`
9. Phase 9: `competitive-analysis-submission`
10. Phase 10: `address-feedback`
11. Phase 11: `retrospective`
