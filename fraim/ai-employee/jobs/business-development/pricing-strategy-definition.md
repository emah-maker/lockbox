# FRAIM Job: pricing-strategy-definition

## Intent
To create a comprehensive pricing and packaging strategy for any product that clarifies who the product is for and what outcomes it delivers, defines SKUs, tiers, add-ons, and the pricing metric, ties pricing to unit economics and scalability, and positions the product clearly against competitors and substitutes.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: a .docx review copy generated from the final artifact via the author-docx skill at submission; this is the outcome delivered for review, with the .md below as the canonical agent-side record.
- Final artifact: A comprehensive `docs/business-development/pricing-strategy.md` document.
- **Clear Value Proposition**: Pricing and packaging align with customer outcomes.
- **Economic Grounding**: Pricing is profitable, defensible, and scalable.
- **Competitive Positioning**: Product is clearly positioned against competitors.
