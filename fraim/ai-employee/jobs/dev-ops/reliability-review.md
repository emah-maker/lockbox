# FRAIM Job: reliability-review

## Intent
Conduct a quarterly reliability review that gives engineering leadership a data-driven view of SLO health, error budget consumption, runbook coverage, and on-call burden — with a prioritized action plan to address the most impactful gaps.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/dev-ops/reliability-review-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing SLO compliance table, error budget consumption analysis, runbook gap report with scoring, executive summary with RAG status, and prioritized action plan with owners and due dates.
- **SLO Audit**: Assessment of every SLO against its target for the review period.
- **Error Budget Analysis**: Budget consumption trend by SLO with forecast.
- **Runbook Gap Report**: Identified runbook gaps with frequency and impact scoring.
- **Action Plan**: Prioritized findings with owners, severity, and due dates.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `slo-audit`
2. Phase 2: `error-budget-review`
3. Phase 3: `runbook-review`
4. Phase 4: `action-plan`
5. Phase 5: `reliability-review-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
