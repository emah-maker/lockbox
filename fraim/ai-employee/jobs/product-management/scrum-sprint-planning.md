# FRAIM Job: scrum-sprint-planning

## Intent
Run a structured sprint planning process — from backlog grooming through capacity planning, sprint goal definition, and acceptance criteria review — so the team starts the sprint with a clear commitment, not a wish list.

## Outcome
- Final artifact: Sprint plan at `docs/delivery/sprints/{date}-sprint-{number}-plan.md`.
- **Sprint Goal**: A single, measurable outcome statement that defines what the sprint delivers.
- **Sprint Backlog**: A set of issues sized to team capacity with confirmed acceptance criteria.
- **Capacity Plan**: Team velocity and availability factored into the committed work.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `backlog-review`
2. Phase 2: `capacity-planning`
3. Phase 3: `sprint-goal-definition`
4. Phase 4: `acceptance-criteria-review`
5. Phase 5: `sprint-plan-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
