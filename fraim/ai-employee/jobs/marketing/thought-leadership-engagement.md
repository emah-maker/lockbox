# FRAIM Job: thought-leadership-engagement

## Intent
Build strategic credibility and influence by engaging with peers, investors, or customers through high-quality written content. This job coordinates the research, design, and delivery of "thought leadership" (articles, whitepapers, deep-dives) that position the founder/project as a category leader.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/marketing/thought-leadership-engagement-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with published article, strategic thesis, and verified evidence base
- **Strategic Thesis**: A clear explanation of why this piece matters to the target audience and how it supports business goals.
- **Verified Evidence Base**: A collection of data points and case studies that back the central thesis.
