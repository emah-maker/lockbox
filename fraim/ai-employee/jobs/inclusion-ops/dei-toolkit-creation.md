# FRAIM Job: dei-toolkit-creation

## Intent
Generate structured toolkits and ready-to-use templates for ERGs and inclusion committees so inclusion leaders can equip facilitators without starting from a blank page.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/inclusion/toolkits/{topic-slug}-toolkit-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with facilitator guide, timed session flow, discussion prompts with facilitator notes, and 1-pager summary ready for distribution
- A toolkit that contains at minimum: a facilitator guide, discussion prompts, and a standalone 1-pager summary
- Output formatted for immediate distribution to ERG leads
