# FRAIM Job: executive-order-dei-impact-analysis

## Intent
Compare executive orders affecting DEI with EEOC guidance and current best practices, producing a scenario analysis that enables legal counsel and HR leadership to make informed decisions about program adjustments.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/inclusion/regulatory/{order-slug}-impact-analysis-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with side-by-side comparison table of EO provisions vs EEOC guidance vs current programs, three or more scenarios with implications, and legal-review disclaimer on all pages
- A side-by-side comparison table: EO provisions vs. EEOC guidance vs. existing org programs
- Three or more scenarios with implications for the org's DEI programs
- Mandatory legal-review disclaimer on every output page
- Header: `DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW — Not legal advice. Requires human legal counsel review before any action is taken.`

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `ingest-regulatory-documents`
2. Phase 2: `compare-and-contrast`
3. Phase 3: `generate-scenarios`
4. Phase 4: `submit`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
