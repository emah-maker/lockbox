# FRAIM Job: ai-governance-for-inclusion

## Intent
Map where AI makes or influences decisions inside the organization, identify where inclusion review and human accountability are missing, and define an oversight structure with explicit HITL requirements.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/inclusion/governance/ai-decision-map-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with AI workflow inventory classified by decision type, inclusion review gaps, and HITL flag recommendations with internal-review disclaimer
- A decision map classifying every AI-touched workflow as automate / augment / human-only
- Identified missing inclusion review points per workflow
- HITL flag recommendations for decisions that require explicit human accountability
- Header: `INTERNAL — DRAFT FOR REVIEW — Not legal advice; requires human review before action.`
