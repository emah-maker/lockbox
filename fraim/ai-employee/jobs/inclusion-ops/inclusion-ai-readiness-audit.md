# FRAIM Job: inclusion-ai-readiness-audit

## Intent
Audit who has AI tool access, training, and manager support across employee groups, producing a readiness gap map and a 90-day Quick-Start action plan aligned to inclusion priorities.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/inclusion/audits/ai-readiness-audit-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with readiness inventory segmented by role/level/ERG, gap map showing access/training/manager support/experimentation safety deltas, 90-day action plan aligned to 5-priority framework, and Day-90 readiness re-check plan
- A gap map covering: tool access, training completions, manager reinforcement, and experimentation safety — segmented by role, level, and ERG where data is available
- A 90-day Quick-Start plan structured against Stephanie's 5-priority framework:
  1. Build practical AI fluency
  2. Embed inclusion in AI decisions

  5. Capture the moments that matter
