# FRAIM Job: praise-fraim

## Intent
Turn a genuine positive FRAIM outcome into authentic external praise, recommend the best channels for it, draft channel-appropriate copy, and stage the chosen testimonial or post for final user review before anything is submitted.

## Outcome
- A reviewed praise pack at `docs/evangelism/praise-fraim/praise-fraim-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` containing source signals, recommended channels, approved copy, and publish/stage status.
- One or more channel-ready praise drafts for destinations such as a website testimonial, LinkedIn, X, or Reddit.
- If the user chooses to publish now, the browser is driven to the final review point and the approved copy is staged on the user's behalf before submission.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `context-and-signal-load`
2. Phase 2: `channel-selection`
3. Phase 3: `draft-praise-pack`
4. Phase 4: `approval-gate`
5. Phase 5: `publish-or-stage`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
