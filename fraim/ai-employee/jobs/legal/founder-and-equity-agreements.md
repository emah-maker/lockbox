# FRAIM Job: founder-and-equity-agreements

## Intent
Help a founding team agree on and document a fair, durable equity arrangement so the team stays aligned and protected as the company grows.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at `docs/legal/founder-agreements/{company-name}-founder-equity-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx` with the agreed split + framework, vesting terms, roles/decision-rights, a founder-agreement draft, and a counsel-review checklist.
- A handoff to `cap-table-construction` for the ledger.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `founding-team-intake`
2. Phase 2: `equity-split-modeling`
3. Phase 3: `vesting-and-roles-definition`
4. Phase 4: `agreement-drafting`
5. Phase 5: `agreement-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
