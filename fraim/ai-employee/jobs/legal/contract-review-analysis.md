# FRAIM Job: contract-review-analysis

## Intent
Provide thorough, strategic legal contract review that identifies risks, strengthens protections, optimizes business positioning, and when requested produces counterparty-ready redlines. This job enables comprehensive analysis of SOWs, NDAs, Terms & Conditions, and other legal documents to protect interests while maintaining business relationships.

## Outcome
- Final artifact: A comprehensive contract review report with clear action plan and, when requested, an editable counterparty-ready redline.
- **Identified Risks**: Prioritized legal and business risks.
- **Practical Recommendations**: Actionable improvements and negotiation strategies.
- **Counterparty-Ready Redline**: Minimal, relationship-aware redlines that preserve the user's material interests without over-editing.
