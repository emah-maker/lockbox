## Intent
To ensure a smooth and successful feature launch by systematically verifying all marketing, technical, and operational readiness, submitting the launch plan for review, and executing a coordinated, multi-channel launch only after the manager approves.

- **Risk Mitigation**: All high-priority gaps identified and resolved before the first public announcement.
