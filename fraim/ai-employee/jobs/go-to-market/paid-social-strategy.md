# FRAIM Job: paid-social-strategy

## Intent
Design and structure a paid social campaign — on LinkedIn, Meta, or TikTok — that reaches the right audience with the right creative at a defensible cost per result.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/marketing/paid-social/{date}-paid-social-strategy.pptx with Audience Segments (2-4 segments with targeting parameters and reach), Creative Brief (per-segment direction with hook/body/CTA), Campaign Structure (hierarchy with bidding and budget), and Reporting Framework (KPIs, optimization cadence, pause thresholds).
- **Audience Segments**: 2–4 audience segments with targeting parameters and estimated reach.
- **Creative Brief**: Per-segment creative direction with ad format, hook, body, and CTA.
- **Campaign Structure**: Campaign → ad set → ad hierarchy with bidding and budget recommendations.
- **Reporting Framework**: KPIs, optimization cadence, and pause thresholds.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `audience-research`
2. Phase 2: `creative-brief`
3. Phase 3: `campaign-setup`
4. Phase 4: `reporting`
5. Phase 5: `paid-social-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
