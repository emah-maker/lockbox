# FRAIM Job: account-strategy

## Intent
Build an account strategy for a specific customer — assessing health, identifying expansion opportunities, preparing a QBR, and developing a renewal plan — so the account grows and renews rather than churning quietly.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/accounts/{date}-{company}-account-strategy.docx with Health Assessment (score, churn risk, primary risk factor), Expansion Map (opportunities with SPICED evidence), QBR Agenda (talking points), and Renewal Plan (timeline, stakeholder strategy, BATNA).
- **Health Assessment**: Current account health score with churn risk classification.
- **Expansion Map**: Identified expansion opportunities with SPICED-validated signals.
- **QBR Agenda**: Quarterly business review agenda and talking points.
- **Renewal Plan**: Renewal timeline, stakeholder plan, and BATNA.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `health-assessment`
2. Phase 2: `expansion-mapping`
3. Phase 3: `qbr-prep`
4. Phase 4: `renewal-plan`
5. Phase 5: `account-strategy-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
