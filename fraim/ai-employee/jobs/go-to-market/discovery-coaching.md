# FRAIM Job: discovery-coaching

## Intent
Coach a sales rep through a specific discovery call — from pre-call research to SPICED qualification scoring to next-step planning — so the rep enters the call prepared and exits with a qualified (or disqualified) opportunity.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/discovery/{date}-{company}-discovery.docx with Pre-Call Brief (account context, stakeholder research, SPICED-aligned questions), SPICED Scorecard (post-call qualification across all dimensions), and Next Step plan with advancement criteria.
- **Pre-Call Brief**: Account context, stakeholder research, and ≥ 5 SPICED-aligned discovery questions.
- **SPICED Scorecard**: Post-call qualification assessment across all 5 SPICED dimensions.
- **Next Step**: Agreed next step with clear criteria for advancing vs. disqualifying the opportunity.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `prep`
2. Phase 2: `call-execution`
3. Phase 3: `qualification-review`
4. Phase 4: `next-step`
5. Phase 5: `discovery-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
