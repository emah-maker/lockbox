# FRAIM Job: analyze-revenue-system

## Intent
Analyze existing revenue data to produce a diagnostic of what is working and what is not across the full Winning by Design Bowtie — both the acquisition bow (Educate→Sign) and the expansion bow (Onboard→Advocate).

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/gtm/revenue-system-diagnostic-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with ICP Clusters, Bowtie Left Bow Health, Bowtie Right Bow Health, What's Working/What's Not analysis, and Data Quality Flags.
- **ICP Clusters**: Top 2–3 ICP clusters by closed-won volume and ACV with conversion rates and velocity.
- **Bowtie Left Bow Health**: Stage-by-stage conversion rates from Educate through Sign; highest-dollar drop-off stage identified.
- **Bowtie Right Bow Health**: Onboard completion rate, Adopt milestone rates, Expand trigger hit/miss, Renew rate, Advocate rate by ICP cluster.
- **What's Working / What's Not**: Evidence-grounded summary with ≥1 recommendation per section.
- **Data Quality Flags**: Missing fields and low-completion rates called out explicitly — no fabrication.
