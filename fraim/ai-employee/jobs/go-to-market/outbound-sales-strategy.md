# FRAIM Job: outbound-sales-strategy

## Intent
Build an outbound sales strategy that defines the ICP, designs a multi-touch outreach sequence, enables launch, and establishes a review cadence — so the team is consistently generating qualified pipeline rather than reacting to inbound.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/{date}-outbound-sales-strategy.docx with ICP Definition (firmographic and buyer profiles), Outreach Sequences (per-ICP multi-touch with SPICED alignment), Launch Checklist (tools, sequences, pilot cohort), and Review Cadence (weekly metrics, pause thresholds, iteration protocol).
- **ICP Definition**: Firmographic and buyer-level ICP with scoring criteria.
- **Outreach Sequences**: Per-ICP multi-touch sequences (≥ 3 touches, ≥ 2 channels) with SPICED-aligned messaging.
- **Launch Checklist**: Tools configured, sequences loaded, and pilot cohort identified.
- **Review Cadence**: Weekly sequence performance review and iteration protocol.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `icp-definition`
2. Phase 2: `sequence-design`
3. Phase 3: `launch`
4. Phase 4: `review`
5. Phase 5: `outbound-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
