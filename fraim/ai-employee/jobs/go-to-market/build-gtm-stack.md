# FRAIM Job: build-gtm-stack

## Intent
Evaluate, configure, and instrument the GTM technology stack — CRM pipeline stages, outreach sequencing, lead scoring, and funnel event instrumentation — required to execute the GTM motion and measure both bows of the Bowtie.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/gtm-stack-config-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with Stack Recommendation (tool choices, pricing, rationale), CRM Pipeline configuration, Sequences + Lead Scoring model, Instrumentation Spec, and Quarterly Maintenance Checklist.
- **Stack Recommendation**: Tool choices per company ARR stage and motion type, with current pricing (sourced and dated) and rationale.
- **CRM Pipeline**: Stages mapped to the Bowtie journey; required fields enforced; ICP properties added.
- **Sequences + Lead Scoring**: Outreach sequences uploaded as templates; lead scoring model with fit + behavior scores.
- **Instrumentation Spec**: Funnel events (Bowtie stage transitions) defined with name, fire condition, required properties, and stage mapping.
- **Quarterly Maintenance Checklist**: What to audit to keep the stack healthy.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `audit-current-tooling`
2. Phase 2: `recommend-stack`
3. Phase 3: `configure-crm`
4. Phase 4: `configure-sequences-and-scoring`
5. Phase 5: `produce-instrumentation-spec`
6. Phase 6: `submit`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
