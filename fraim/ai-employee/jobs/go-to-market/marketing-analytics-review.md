# FRAIM Job: marketing-analytics-review

## Intent
Conduct a comprehensive marketing analytics review that assesses funnel performance by channel, computes true attribution, and delivers prioritized optimization recommendations grounded in data rather than platform-reported vanity metrics.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/marketing/analytics/{date}-marketing-analytics-review.pptx with Full Funnel Metrics by channel, Channel Attribution (true CPA vs platform CPA), Performance Analysis (over/underperformers), and Optimization Recommendations with expected impact and effort.
- **Funnel Metrics**: Full-funnel view from impression to revenue by channel.
- **Channel Attribution**: True CPA and CAC per channel with attribution model documented.
- **Performance Analysis**: Identification of underperforming and overperforming channels.
- **Optimization Recommendations**: Prioritized actions with expected impact and effort.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `data-collection`
2. Phase 2: `channel-attribution`
3. Phase 3: `performance-analysis`
4. Phase 4: `optimization-recommendation`
5. Phase 5: `marketing-analytics-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
