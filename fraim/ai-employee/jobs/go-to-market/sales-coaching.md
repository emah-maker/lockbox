# FRAIM Job: sales-coaching

## Intent
Coach a sales rep — through call review, playbook guidance, and skill gap tracking — to improve SPICED qualification discipline, discovery depth, and deal progression, measured in win rate and average deal cycle improvement.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/coaching/{date}-{rep-name}-coaching.docx with Rep Assessment (performance metrics, strengths, development areas), Call Review (SPICED-grounded feedback on call execution), Playbook Guidance (skill focus with scripts and practice exercises), and Skill Gap Tracking with progress metrics.
- **Rep Assessment**: Strengths, development areas, and current performance metrics.
- **Call Review**: Specific call-level feedback grounded in SPICED criteria.
- **Skill Gap Tracking**: Identified skill gaps with a focused development plan.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `rep-assessment`
2. Phase 2: `call-review`
3. Phase 3: `playbook-guidance`
4. Phase 4: `skill-gap-tracking`
5. Phase 5: `coaching-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
