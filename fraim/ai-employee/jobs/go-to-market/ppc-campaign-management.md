# FRAIM Job: ppc-campaign-management

## Intent
Audit, restructure, and optimize a paid search (Google Ads / Microsoft Ads) account to reduce wasted spend, improve Quality Score, and drive more qualified conversions at a lower CPA.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/marketing/ppc/{date}-ppc-campaign-report.pptx with Account Audit (waste identification, opportunity sizing), Account Structure recommendation, Quality Score Governance plan with compliance gate, and Performance Report (baseline KPIs and post-optimization projections).
- **Account Audit**: Current state assessment with waste identification and opportunity sizing.
- **Account Structure**: Recommended campaign/ad group structure with keyword theming.
- **Quality Score Governance**: Quality Score ≥ 7 for ≥ 70% of spend-weighted keywords — this is the quality gate for this job.
- **Performance Report**: Baseline KPIs and post-optimization projection.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `audit`
2. Phase 2: `account-structure-design`
3. Phase 3: `bidding-strategy-selection`
4. Phase 4: `quality-score-governance`
5. Phase 5: `performance-reporting`
6. Phase 6: `ppc-submission`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
