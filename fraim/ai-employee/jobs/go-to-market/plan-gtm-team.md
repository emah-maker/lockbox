# FRAIM Job: plan-gtm-team

## Intent
Produce a sequenced GTM team hiring plan anchored in the company's current ARR stage and motion type — recommending which roles to hire first, at which revenue milestones, and what to look for when hiring each role.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/gtm-team-hiring-plan-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with Sequenced Role Recommendations (roles in hiring order anchored to ARR triggers), Per-Role Briefs (what role unlocks, profile, interview questions), Compensation Benchmarks (sourced and dated), and Handoff to role-intake-and-scorecard recommendation.
- **Sequenced Role Recommendations**: ≥3 roles in hiring order, each anchored to a specific ARR trigger milestone.
- **Per-Role Brief**: What the role unlocks, sequencing rationale, profile (experience level + ≥3 competencies), ≥3 interview questions calibrated to the company's motion, compensation benchmark (sourced and dated).
- **Handoff to `role-intake-and-scorecard`**: For the immediate next hire, a recommendation to run `role-intake-and-scorecard` with the brief as input.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `assess-revenue-stage`
2. Phase 2: `sequence-hiring-plan`
3. Phase 3: `research-compensation`
4. Phase 4: `produce-hiring-plan`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
