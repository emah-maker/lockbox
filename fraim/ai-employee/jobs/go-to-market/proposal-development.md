# FRAIM Job: proposal-development

## Intent
Build a compelling, deal-specific proposal that reflects the prospect's stated needs, demonstrates ROI in their terms, and gives their internal champion the ammunition to build the business case for approval.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/proposals/{date}-{company}-proposal.docx with Discovery Synthesis (prospect's pain/impact/outcome in their words), Solution Design (scoped recommendation mapped to needs), ROI Model (quantified business case with prospect's numbers), and Executive Summary for economic buyer.
- **Discovery Synthesis**: The prospect's pain, impact, and expected outcome — in their words.
- **Solution Design**: Scoped recommendation mapped to their specific needs.
- **ROI Model**: Quantified business case using their numbers where available.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `discovery-synthesis`
2. Phase 2: `solution-design`
3. Phase 3: `roi-modeling`
4. Phase 4: `doc-creation`
5. Phase 5: `proposal-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
