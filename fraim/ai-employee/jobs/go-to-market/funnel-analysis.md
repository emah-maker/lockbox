# FRAIM Job: funnel-analysis

## Intent
Produce an actionable AARRR funnel report that identifies the single highest-leverage drop-off stage and delivers prioritized interventions to improve growth metrics.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/gtm/funnel-analysis-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with AARRR Conversion Breakdown (stage-by-stage rates), Retention Cohort Curves (D1/D7/D30), Growth Curves (MoM/WoW trends), Highest-Leverage Drop-Off identification, and Intervention Recommendations with expected impact.
- **AARRR Conversion Breakdown**: Conversion rates across every stage — Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Referral → Revenue.
- **Retention Cohort Curves**: Day 1, Day 7, and Day 30 retention plotted by acquisition cohort.
- **Growth Curves**: MoM/WoW user growth, revenue growth, and activation rate trend.
- **Highest-Leverage Drop-Off**: The single stage with the greatest impact on overall growth if improved.
- **Intervention Recommendations**: At least 3 concrete interventions with expected impact framing for the identified drop-off stage.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `pull-funnel-data`
2. Phase 2: `compute-aarrr-conversion-rates`
3. Phase 3: `build-retention-cohort-curves`
4. Phase 4: `identify-drop-off-and-recommend-interventions`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
