# FRAIM Job: seo-strategy

## Intent
Build an SEO strategy that identifies high-value keyword opportunities, fixes technical barriers to ranking, closes content gaps, and delivers a prioritized roadmap — so organic traffic becomes a predictable, compounding growth channel.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/marketing/seo/{date}-seo-strategy.pptx with Keyword Opportunity Set (prioritized by business value), Technical Audit (issues ranked by ranking impact), Content Gap Report (missing topics competitors rank for), and 90-Day Roadmap (prioritized actions with effort and impact).
- **Keyword Opportunity Set**: Prioritized keyword list with volume, difficulty, and business value.
- **Technical Audit**: Technical SEO issues ranked by ranking impact.
- **Content Gap Report**: Content topics missing from the site that competitors rank for.
- **90-Day Roadmap**: Prioritized actions for technical fixes, content creation, and link building.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `keyword-research`
2. Phase 2: `technical-audit`
3. Phase 3: `content-gap-analysis`
4. Phase 4: `roadmap`
5. Phase 5: `seo-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
