# FRAIM Job: ad-performance-analysis

## Intent
Analyze cross-channel ad performance to identify what's working, what's wasting budget, and what to do next — producing a prioritized optimization plan grounded in attribution data, not platform-reported vanity metrics.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/marketing/paid/{date}-ad-performance-analysis.pptx with Channel Attribution tables, Creative Performance rankings, and Optimization Plan with expected impact projections.
- **Channel Attribution**: Performance by channel with attributed conversions and true CPA (not last-click).
- **Creative Performance**: Best and worst performing creative assets with statistical significance note.
- **Optimization Plan**: Prioritized list of budget shifts, pauses, and tests with expected impact.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `data-collection`
2. Phase 2: `attribution`
3. Phase 3: `optimization-recommendation`
4. Phase 4: `ad-performance-submission`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
