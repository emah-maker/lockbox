# FRAIM Job: mobile-app-submission

## Intent
Submit a mobile binary to the destination app store. Single job parameterized by `platform` — composes the App Store Connect (iOS) or Google Play Console (Android) flow based on a Phase 1 decision. Reused on every release. Covers the store-side end-to-end: metadata, screenshots, privacy disclosures, demo creds (where applicable), reviewer notes, and the actual Submit-for-Review click. Routes any rejection into `mobile-app-rejection-response`.

## Outcome
- **Submitted Version**: A store version is in `Waiting for Review` / `In Review` / `In review` status with all required fields filled and one binary attached.
- **Demo-Ready Account** (sign-in apps only): Synthetic reviewer account exists and is seeded with realistic data.
- **Submission Audit Trail**: A submission ID, build/version code, and complete log of fields filled in the project's evidence dir.
- **Monitoring Hook**: Either approval is recorded, or the rejection routes into `mobile-app-rejection-response` with full context preserved.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `platform-selection`
2. Phase 2: `policy-preflight`
3. Phase 3: `metadata-authoring`
4. Phase 4: `screenshots-and-graphics`
5. Phase 5: `privacy-disclosure`
6. Phase 6: `store-specific-fields`
7. Phase 7: `demo-account-and-review-info`
8. Phase 8: `submit-for-review`
9. Phase 9: `monitor-and-respond`
