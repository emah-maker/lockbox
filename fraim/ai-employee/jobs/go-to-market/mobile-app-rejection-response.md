# FRAIM Job: mobile-app-rejection-response

## Intent
Respond to an app store rejection — analyze the cited guideline / policy, gather evidence, draft a Resolution Center / Policy Center reply, post it, and decide next steps (clarify-and-resubmit, ship a fixed binary, or escalate via App Review Board / Play appeal). Single job parameterized by `platform` — composes Apple-specific or Play-specific guidance based on a Phase 1 decision. Reused on every rejection cycle. Triggered from `mobile-app-submission` → `monitor-and-respond` when a rejection is received.

## Outcome
- **A Posted Reply**: A Resolution Center (iOS) or Policy Center (Android) message responding to the rejection is sent.
- **A Clear Decision**: One of three paths chosen and acted on: (a) Clarify-and-Resubmit, (b) Ship a Fixed Binary, (c) Escalate (App Review Board / Play appeal).
- **Audit Trail**: The full rejection thread, evidence collected, and decision rationale captured in `docs/<store>/rejections/<submission-id>.md`.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `platform-selection`
2. Phase 2: `rejection-analysis`
3. Phase 3: `evidence-gathering`
4. Phase 4: `response-drafting`
5. Phase 5: `post-reply`
6. Phase 6: `re-evaluate-resubmit-or-appeal`
