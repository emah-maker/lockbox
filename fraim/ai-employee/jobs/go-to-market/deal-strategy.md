# FRAIM Job: deal-strategy

## Intent
Build a deal strategy for a specific active opportunity — assessing deal health, mapping the buying committee, and producing a negotiation plan that advances the deal to close with appropriate deal terms.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/deals/{date}-{company}-deal-strategy.docx with Opportunity Assessment (SPICED score and risk flags), Stakeholder Map (buying committee and influence levels), and Negotiation Plan (pricing range, concession sequencing, mutual close plan).
- **Opportunity Assessment**: Deal health score against SPICED criteria with risk flags.
- **Stakeholder Map**: Buying committee mapped with champion, economic buyer, champion, and blockers identified.
- **Negotiation Plan**: Approved-range pricing, concession sequencing, and mutual close plan.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `opportunity-assessment`
2. Phase 2: `stakeholder-mapping`
3. Phase 3: `negotiation-plan`
4. Phase 4: `deal-strategy-submission`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
