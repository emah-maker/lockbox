# FRAIM Job: marketing-strategy-definition

## Intent
To define the value proposition, target audience, and launch strategy for new features, ensuring clear communication of benefits and effective market entry.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/marketing/strategy/{issue_number}-{feature-name}.docx with Clear Value Proposition, Defined Target Audience (persona and pain points), Channel Strategy (communication channels and tone), and Launch recommendations.
- **Clear Value Proposition**: Concise statement of feature benefits.
- **Defined Target Audience**: Specific persona and their pain points.
- **Channel Strategy**: Recommended communication channels and tone.
