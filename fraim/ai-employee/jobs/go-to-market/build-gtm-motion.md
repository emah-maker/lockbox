# FRAIM Job: build-gtm-motion

## Intent
Build the GTM motion playbooks for each priority ICP: structured guides covering outreach through close using SPICED discovery, and expansion trigger playbooks covering the right bow of the Bowtie.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/gtm/gtm-motion-playbook-{icp-label}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with Prospecting Criteria, Outreach Sequence templates, SPICED Discovery Framework, Objection Handling Guide with responses, and Expansion Trigger Playbook.
- **Prospecting Criteria**: Exact firmographic and trigger-event filters for list building.
- **Outreach Sequence**: ≥3 ICP-specific message templates with timing across ≥2 channels.
- **SPICED Discovery Framework**: All 5 dimensions with ICP-tailored questions and a qualification gate.
- **Objection Handling Guide**: ≥3 objections with responses grounded in revenue diagnostic data, or labeled representative if no data is available.
- **Expansion Trigger Playbook**: ≥2 expansion signals with corresponding CS/AE actions.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `load-icp-context`
2. Phase 2: `build-outreach-sequences`
3. Phase 3: `build-spiced-discovery`
4. Phase 4: `build-objection-guide`
5. Phase 5: `build-expansion-playbook`
6. Phase 6: `produce-playbook`
7. Phase 7: `submit`
8. Phase 8: `address-feedback`
9. Phase 9: `retrospective`
