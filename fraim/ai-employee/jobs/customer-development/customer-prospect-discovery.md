# FRAIM Job: customer-prospect-discovery

## Intent
Build a comprehensive database of qualified prospects who have explicitly expressed pain points that the product solves. This job systematically discovers potential customers across multiple platforms (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Reddit) and creates a prioritized prospect inventory for future outreach efforts.

**When to use**: When you need to discover who your potential customers are and where they express relevant pain points online. Run this before participant recruitment to build your prospect database.

## Outcome
- **Primary artifacts**: Dated run artifacts in `docs/customer-development/customer-prospect-discovery/`:
  - `customer-prospect-discovery-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` with the run context, ICP snapshot, search strategy, discovery notes, lighthouse prospects, and recommended outreach angles
  - `prospects-{YYYY-MM-DD}.csv` containing 50-200 qualified prospects with contact information and pain-point evidence
- **Reviewable deliverable**: a .docx review copy generated from the run dossier via the author-docx skill at submission; the dossier .md remains the canonical record.
- **Run artifact boundary**: Those two files are the only customer-development run artifacts this job may create. Do not create separate `social-prospects-*`, `qualified-prospects-*`, `events-*`, `target-accounts-*`, `customer-persona-*`, `search-strategy-*`, `discovery-summary-*`, `inventory-compilation-*`, or `lighthouse-prospects-*` files. Put event maps, target-account maps, search strategy, social-only notes, dropped prospects, and lighthouse summaries into the dossier sections.
- **Search Strategy Dossier**: The bundle dossier documents the map of keywords, target communities, and search queries for repeatable discovery across platforms.
- **Qualified Lead Set**: A ranked list of individuals with verified pain-point evidence, scored by relevance (1-10).
- **Lighthouse Prospects**: Top 3-5 highest-priority prospects identified for immediate outreach.
- **Delivery audit trail**: Generic FRAIM evidence and retrospective files may be created by delivery phases, but they are audit artifacts, not customer-development run artifacts.

**Next step**: Use `participant-recruitment` job to convert these prospects into interview participants.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `context-synthesis`
2. Phase 2: `customer-profiling`
3. Phase 3: `platform-discovery`
4. Phase 4: `prospect-qualification`
5. Phase 5: `inventory-compilation`
6. Phase 6: `prospect-submission`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
