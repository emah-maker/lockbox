# FRAIM Job: review-customer-development

## Intent
Holistically evaluate the founder's customer-development artifact set — prospect discovery, interview prep, participant recruitment, processed interview notes, user surveys, and triaged customer needs — and produce a portfolio-level quality score for the founder-journey quality dashboard. This job does not create customer-development artifacts; it reviews what exists and tells the founder how well the customer-development surface is actually being covered.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: a .pptx synthesis deck generated from the review artifact at submission; this is the outcome delivered for review, with the .md below as the canonical agent-side record.
- **Final artifact**: `docs/customer-development/customer-development-quality-review.md`
- **Quality Scores**: Flat sub-scores (0-10) for ICP coherence, interview coverage, evidence quality, pattern saturation, signal-to-product flow, and survey rigor.
- **Composite Score**: Overall customer-development quality (0-10), emitted to `fraim_quality_scores`.
- **Coaching**: Specific, actionable recommendations for the highest-impact improvements.
- **Gap Analysis**: What's missing or weak across the customer-development artifact set.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `artifact-discovery`
2. Phase 2: `prospect-and-icp-review`
3. Phase 3: `interview-coverage-review`
4. Phase 4: `pattern-saturation-review`
5. Phase 5: `signal-to-product-review`
6. Phase 6: `cross-artifact-coherence`
7. Phase 7: `quality-score-emission`
8. Phase 8: `review-submission`
