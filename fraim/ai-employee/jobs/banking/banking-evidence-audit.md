# FRAIM Job: banking-evidence-audit

## Intent
Audit a prior banking KYC job by reviewing its evidence, receipt, exceptions, and decision trace for completeness and defensibility.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A human-readable audit report at `docs/banking/{case-id}/{case-id}-{YYYY-MM-DD}-audit-report.docx`.
- **Evidence review**: A checklist mapping each KYC requirement to the artifact and evidence id that supports it.
- **Exception report**: Any missing, stale, contradictory, or unverifiable evidence is clearly listed.
- **Audit decision**: A pass, pass-with-observations, or fail conclusion tied to the reviewed KYC case.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `audit-scope`
2. Phase 2: `evidence-review`
3. Phase 3: `exception-analysis`
4. Phase 4: `audit-decision`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
