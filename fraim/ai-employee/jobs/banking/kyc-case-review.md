# FRAIM Job: kyc-case-review

## Intent
Run a banking KYC case review from the case facts the manager provides, applying standard KYC defaults and producing durable evidence that a future auditor can inspect.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A human-readable KYC review artifact at `docs/banking/{case-id}/{case-id}-{YYYY-MM-DD}-kyc-review.docx`.
- **KYC decision**: A clear approve, reject, or needs-human-review determination with reasons.
- **Evidence pack**: A readable Word artifact tying the decision to evidence ids, source notes, observations, and a receipt.
- **Audit handoff**: Stable artifact paths that `banking-evidence-audit` can consume later.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `case-intake`
2. Phase 2: `evidence-collection`
3. Phase 3: `kyc-decision`
4. Phase 4: `evidence-package`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
