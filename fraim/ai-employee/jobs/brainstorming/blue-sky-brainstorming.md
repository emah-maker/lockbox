# FRAIM Job: blue-sky-brainstorming

## Intent
Generate visionary, moonshot, and futuristic ideas without the constraints of current implementation details. Ask: *"What **could** we build if anything were possible?"* (Top-down, unconstrained imagination). This is for exploring "what if" scenarios and breakthrough 10x possibilities — not incremental improvements.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/brainstorming/blue-sky-brainstorming-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing moonshot ideas organized by dimension (technology, UX, architecture, business model), future scenarios with vision statements and impact assessments, and reality bridges mapping visionary concepts to near-term proofs of concept.
- **Breakthrough Concepts**: Documented ideas for 10x improvements across technology, UX, architecture, and business model.
- **Strategic Inspiration**: Foundational material for roadmap planning and experimental proofs of concept.

## Steps

3. Phase 3: `scenario-development`

5. Phase 5: `compile-and-review-blue-sky-vision`

7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
