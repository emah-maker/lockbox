# FRAIM Job: offer-negotiation

## Intent
Evaluate an offer against market benchmarks, build a negotiation strategy, and produce a step-by-step script for landing the best possible title, compensation, and terms — with word-for-word language for every stage of the conversation.

## Outcome
- An `Offer Negotiation Plan` DOCX at `docs/career/{slug}-offer-negotiation-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx`, marked `PERSONAL - CAREER CONFIDENTIAL` and `DRAFT - Review before use`
- Canonical markdown source at `docs/career/{slug}-offer-negotiation-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`, generated before the DOCX export
- Salary benchmarks at P25/P50/P75, sourced and dated
- Negotiation strategy: opening ask, walk-away floor, concessions, competing offer framing guidance
- Word-for-word verbal response script and counter-offer email template
- Pushback handling scripts for "this is our best offer" and "our bands are fixed"
- Offer lifecycle tracker: received → countered → final outcome
- Artifact headers: `PERSONAL — CAREER CONFIDENTIAL` and `DRAFT — Review before use in any conversation`

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `gather-offer-details`
2. Phase 2: `research-benchmarks`
3. Phase 3: `build-negotiation-strategy`
4. Phase 4: `submit`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
