# FRAIM Job: career-trajectory-planning

## Intent
Research the job market and design a 3-horizon career path grounded in the user's positioning, open roles, salary benchmarks, and industry trends.

## Outcome
- A `Career Path Plan` DOCX at `docs/career/{slug}-trajectory-plan-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx`, marked `PERSONAL - CAREER CONFIDENTIAL`
- Canonical markdown source at `docs/career/{slug}-trajectory-plan-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`, generated before the DOCX export
- Salary benchmarks at P25/P50/P75 for the H1 target role, sourced and dated
- A 3-horizon plan covering 0–6, 6–18, and 18–36 months, each with a target role and company profile
- A company shortlist of 10-15 companies for Horizon 1 with a fit-alignment rationale for each
- A gap bridge plan per horizon: skills to develop, experiences to collect, and a target completion date
- All unverified or user-supplied inputs labeled explicitly

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `gather-positioning`
2. Phase 2: `research-market`
3. Phase 3: `build-trajectory`
4. Phase 4: `submit`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
