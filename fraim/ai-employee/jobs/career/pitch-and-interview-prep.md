# FRAIM Job: pitch-and-interview-prep

## Intent
Prepare the user to pitch themselves and ace interviews at a specific company and role, with tailored questions, answers, and a one-page pre-interview card they can review 30 minutes before the call.

## Outcome
- A `Job Interview Prep` DOCX at `docs/career/{slug}-pitch-and-interview-prep-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx`
- Canonical markdown source at `docs/career/{slug}-pitch-and-interview-prep-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`, generated before the DOCX export
- A 30-second pitch and 2-minute "tell me about yourself" tailored to the company's current priorities
- >=10 predicted interview questions with suggested answers in STAR or problem-solution-impact format
- Per-interviewer research section with likely focus areas (when names are provided or can be inferred)
- Format-specific content: behavioral story bank, case framework (PM/strategy roles), or technical refresher (engineering roles)
- A one-page pre-interview card
- Practice tool recommendations: Yoodli for delivery, Big Interview for mock Q&A
- All company facts sourced from public web and labeled

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `gather-interview-context`
2. Phase 2: `research-company-and-interviewer`
3. Phase 3: `build-prep-materials`
4. Phase 4: `submit`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
