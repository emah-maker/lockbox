# FRAIM Job: sf-build-dashboard

## Intent
Turn a business requirement into a working Salesforce dashboard — query the right objects, build the supporting reports, assemble the dashboard, and validate that every tile loads with real data.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable:** A .pptx presentation at docs/salesforce/sf-build-dashboard-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing dashboard tiles with KPI charts, report summaries, validation results, and data dependencies
- Report(s) created or updated in Salesforce
- Dashboard created or updated and verified to load without errors
- Business stakeholder confirmed each KPI appears on the correct tile
