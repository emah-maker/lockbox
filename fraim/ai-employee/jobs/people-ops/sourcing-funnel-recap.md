# FRAIM Job: sourcing-funnel-recap

## Intent
Give a recruiter a read-only portfolio view across all their open reqs - outreach funnel metrics, top-three focus recommendations, and a hiring-leader-ready DOCX recap - in one command.

## Outcome
- **Chat summary**: top-three focus reqs with metric-based rationales and a compact all-req table.
- **Markdown source**: `docs/people-ops/sourcing-funnel-recap-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` - canonical source for the recap.
- **DOCX recap**: `docs/people-ops/sourcing-funnel-recap-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx` - same-basename export for hiring-leader review and comments.
- **Report template**: canonical Markdown source must be populated from `templates/people-ops/SOURCING-FUNNEL-RECAP-TEMPLATE.md`.
- **Zero write actions**: no sends, enrollments, candidate edits, req updates, or sequence changes.
