# FRAIM Job: candidate-pipeline-management

## Intent
Give the hiring team a clear picture of pipeline health with stale-candidate flags and concrete next-action recommendations for every active candidate.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable:** A .pptx presentation at docs/people-ops/candidate-pipeline-management-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with pipeline state snapshot, stale-candidate flags with next-action recommendations, conversion rates by stage (including sourcing-to-ATS conversion when a LeadGen tool is configured), and overall pipeline health assessment (Green/Yellow/Red)
- A full pipeline state snapshot: candidate name, current stage, last activity date, next step
- Stale-candidate flags per stage using configurable thresholds (default: sourcing 14d, screen 5d, interview 3d, offer 1d)
- Per-candidate next-action recommendations
- Pipeline health summary with conversion rates by stage; sourcing-to-ATS conversion rate when a LeadGen tool is configured

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `connect-recruiting`
2. Phase 2: `load-pipeline-state`
3. Phase 3: `flag-stale-candidates`
4. Phase 4: `produce-pipeline-report`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
