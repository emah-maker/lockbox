# FRAIM Job: payroll-coordination

## Intent
Review payroll accuracy and surface anomalies for HR and finance sign-off before the payroll run executes, reducing the risk of costly corrections after funds are disbursed.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable:** A .pptx presentation at docs/people-ops/payroll-coordination-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with payroll run summary (gross, net, taxes), HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW severity anomalies with context, and pre-approval checklist with sign-off gates
- A pre-approval checklist of anomalies and outstanding items requiring HR/finance sign-off before submission
- All artifacts carry headers: `INTERNAL — HR CONFIDENTIAL` and `DRAFT — Requires Human Approval`

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `pull-payroll-summary`
2. Phase 2: `flag-anomalies`
3. Phase 3: `produce-preapproval-checklist`
4. Phase 4: `submit`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
