# FRAIM Job: offer-and-close-management

## Intent
Arm the hiring manager with a negotiation strategy and offer letter, then track the offer through close so every candidate gets a clear decision and the pipeline record stays current.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable:** A .docx document at docs/people-ops/offer-and-close-management-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with candidate research summary, leverage assessment, negotiation strategy and call guide, offer letter draft, and offer status tracking through closure
- Candidate research summary: current employer, title, tenure, likely comp expectations
- Candidate leverage assessment: competing offers, urgency signals, pipeline tenure
- Negotiation strategy: opening offer, walk-away floor, available concessions, expected counter scenarios
- Step-by-step verbal offer call guide for the hiring manager
- Offer letter draft (base, equity, bonus, start date)
- Offer status tracking through: sent → verbal accepted → written accepted or declined
- ATS and pipeline report updated with the outcome

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `connect-recruiting`
2. Phase 2: `research-candidate`
3. Phase 3: `develop-negotiation-strategy`
4. Phase 4: `draft-offer`
5. Phase 5: `track-offer-to-close`
6. Phase 6: `submit`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
