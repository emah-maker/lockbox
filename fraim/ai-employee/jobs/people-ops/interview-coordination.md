# FRAIM Job: interview-coordination

## Intent
Build a complete interview plan with competency coverage, bar raiser designation, and a post-loop hiring recommendation so the hiring team makes a consistent, evidence-based decision.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable:** A .docx document at docs/people-ops/interview-coordination-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with competency-to-interviewer map, bar raiser designation, question bank (behavioral and situational), interviewer feedback summary, and post-loop hiring recommendation with signal strength per competency
- A competency-to-interviewer map with no uncovered dimensions
- Bar raiser designation with explicit criteria and veto rights
- ≥2 behavioral + ≥1 situational question per competency
- Post-loop hiring recommendation with signal strength per competency

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `map-competencies-to-interviewers`
2. Phase 2: `draft-interview-questions`
3. Phase 3: `collect-debrief-feedback`
4. Phase 4: `synthesize-hiring-recommendation`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
