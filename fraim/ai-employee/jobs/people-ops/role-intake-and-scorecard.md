# FRAIM Job: role-intake-and-scorecard

## Intent
Convert a hiring need into a structured job brief with market research and a competency scorecard the hiring team can use immediately.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable:** A .docx document at docs/people-ops/role-intake-and-scorecard-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with role brief (title, responsibilities, must-haves, nice-to-haves, comp range), market research (talent availability, comp benchmarks, title norms), hiring difficulty estimate (EASY/MEDIUM/HARD), and competency scorecard with ≥3 dimensions and behavioral indicators
- A complete role brief with title, responsibilities, must-haves, nice-to-haves, and comp range
- Market research covering location availability, comp benchmarks, and title norms
- Hiring confidence estimate (EASY / MEDIUM / HARD) based on requirements vs. market data
- Competency scorecard with ≥3 dimensions, each with observable behavioral indicators

2. Phase 2: `research-talent-market`
3. Phase 3: `produce-scorecard`
4. Phase 4: `submit`
