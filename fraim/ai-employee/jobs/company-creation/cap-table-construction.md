# FRAIM Job: cap-table-construction

## Intent
Give a founder an accurate, fully-diluted capitalization table they can trust for incorporation, fundraising, and equity grants.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at `docs/company-creation/cap-table/{company-name}-cap-table-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx` with the fully-diluted table, option pool, conversion scenarios, and a dilution narrative, plus a link to the maintained spreadsheet.
- Context carried forward to fundraising and equity-agreement jobs.

4. Phase 4: `cap-table-assembly`
5. Phase 5: `cap-table-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
