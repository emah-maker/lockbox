# FRAIM Job: google-workspace-setup

## Intent
Provision a Google Workspace tenant on the company's primary domain end-to-end — sign up, verify the domain, publish mail and auth DNS records, harden the admin baseline, and create users, groups, and a shared drive ready for the founding team.

## Outcome
- A provisioned Workspace tenant on the chosen tier with billing configured.
- Domain verified, MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC records published, mail delivery confirmed.
- Admin baseline enforced: 2SV required, hardware keys on super admins, advanced threat protection on, context-aware access default applied.
- At least two super admins, founding-team user accounts created, groups and aliases created, shared drive initialized.
- Execution log at `docs/go-to-market/presence/google-workspace/{company-name}-workspace-setup-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`.
