# FRAIM Job: github-org-setup

## Intent
Create and harden a GitHub organization end-to-end — reserve the slug, verify the company domain, apply the security baseline, invite founding members at their intended roles, and codify repo policy defaults.

## Outcome
- A live org at `github.com/{orgSlug}` on the approved plan with billing configured.
- Domain verified at the org level via published DNS TXT record.
- Security baseline enforced: 2FA required, secret scanning and push protection on, Dependabot on, workflow permissions read-only by default.
- At least two Owners active; member invites sent with correct roles.
- Org-level repo policy defaults saved.
- Execution log at `docs/go-to-market/presence/github/{company-name}-org-setup-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`.
