# FRAIM Job: investor-pitch-preparation

## Intent
Transform raw business strategy and traction data into a high-impact investor pitch package including comprehensive market analysis, financial modeling, narrative story arc, slide-by-slide deck blueprint, and pressure-test Q&A guide. 

This job builds upon business validation findings to create investment-ready presentations for VCs, board members, and external stakeholders.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/fundraising/pitch-package-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing comprehensive market analysis, financial modeling, narrative story arc, slide-by-slide deck blueprint, investor Q&A guide, and executive-level rebuttal frameworks.
- **`docs/fundraising/pitch-package-{timestamp}.md`**: A comprehensive, investment-ready presentation document.
- **Market Analysis**: Detailed TAM/SAM/SOM analysis with competitive positioning
- **Financial Modeling**: Complete P&L breakdown, unit economics, and scenario planning
- **Investment Case**: Clear value proposition with risk mitigation strategies
- **Narrative Alignment**: Strategy reframed into a compelling "Why Now" story.
- **Founder Confidence**: Defensive vs. Offensive Q&A frameworks with objection handling.
