# FRAIM Job: review-funding-preparation

## Intent
Provide a critical, cold-eyed audit of any existing funding preparation (Pitch Deck, Campaign, Grant) to identify weak points, logical fallacies, and readiness gaps.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/fundraising/readiness-audit-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with readiness audit report including strengths, weaknesses, stop/go recommendations, stress-test results, and actionable gap list.
- **Readiness Audit Report**: A breakdown of Strengths, Weaknesses, and "Stop/Go" recommendations.
- **Stress-Test Results**: Identification of "The Question that kills the deal."
- **Actionable Gap List**: Priority list of fixes required before submission.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `scoping`
2. Phase 2: `package-audit`
3. Phase 3: `benchmark-analysis`
4. Phase 4: `readiness-score-delivery`
5. Phase 5: `retrospective`
