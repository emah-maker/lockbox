# FRAIM Job: support-queue-management

## Intent
Monitor, triage, and resolve support conversations — classifying intent, drafting replies, and routing escalations — so the CS team clears the queue with AI assistance while keeping a human in the loop for critical cases.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/customer-success/support-queue-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with sections: Queue Summary (breakdown by priority and resolution path), Actions Taken (conversation IDs, intents, actions), Errors, and Annotations Written
- All open conversations triaged and classified by priority
- AI-drafted replies sent for L1 conversations after user confirmation
- L2/L3 conversations routed to human queue with a handoff note
- Conversation statuses updated in the CRM

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `connect`
2. Phase 2: `load-queue`
3. Phase 3: `triage-and-classify`
4. Phase 4: `draft-replies`
5. Phase 5: `send-and-update`
6. Phase 6: `produce-summary`
7. Phase 7: `submit`
