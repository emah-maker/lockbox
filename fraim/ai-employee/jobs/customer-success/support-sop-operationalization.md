# FRAIM Job: support-sop-operationalization

## Intent
Convert an enterprise Tier 1 support SOP into an approved support playbook that an AI support employee can use to choose the right ticket path, validate policy deterministically, and execute only the allowed adapters, scripts, messages, or escalation steps.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A compact approved playbook JSON at `playbooks/support/{playbook-id}.json`, plus a human-readable SOP interpretation at `docs/evidence/support/{playbook-id}-sop-interpretation.md`
- Ticket decision guidance based on intent definitions, examples, negative examples, required facts, and disambiguation rules
- Deterministic execution policy containing preconditions, prohibited actions, allowed actions, escalation rules, and evidence requirements
- Adapter bindings for supported actions such as ServiceNow ticket updates and M365 password reset
- Test fixtures that prove playbook decisions, missing-fact handling, escalation, and allowed-action selection

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `ingest-sop`
2. Phase 2: `deconstruct-sop`
3. Phase 3: `author-playbook`
4. Phase 4: `bind-adapters`
5. Phase 5: `define-validation`
6. Phase 6: `produce-review-package`
7. Phase 7: `submit`
8. Phase 8: `address-feedback`
9. Phase 9: `retrospective`
