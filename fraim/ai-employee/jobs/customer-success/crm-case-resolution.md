# FRAIM Job: crm-case-resolution

## Intent
Resolve support cases end-to-end: receive case events, triage by priority and type, route to the right owner or queue, draft customer replies, and auto-resolve or escalate based on case complexity.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/customer-success/crm-case-resolution-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with sections: Cases Received, Triage Classification, Actions Taken, Auto-Resolved Cases, Escalations, and Errors
- Updated case/conversation records (status, owner, AI-drafted replies or handoff notes)
- Per-case action log with triage classification, actions taken, and resolution outcome

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `connect`
2. Phase 2: `receive-case-event`
3. Phase 3: `triage-and-route`
4. Phase 4: `resolve-or-escalate`
5. Phase 5: `produce-summary`
6. Phase 6: `submit`
