# FRAIM Job: customer-health-review

## Intent
Surface at-risk customers, accounts, segments, and expansion opportunities so customer success teams can focus on the highest-impact actions - reducing churn and growing NRR.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at `docs/customer-success/customer-health-review-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx` containing portfolio health snapshot, risk distribution, revenue or segment impact where available, at-risk customers/accounts/segments, expansion opportunities, anomaly analysis, and CRM update status.
- Source-mode support: `crm-account`, `segment`, or `mixed`.
- CRM writeback for top-priority account or member records when the configured CRM adapter supports it.
- No PII, payment details, raw case content, or raw conversation content in the artifact.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `connect`
2. Phase 2: `select-source-mode`
3. Phase 3: `load-health-signals`
4. Phase 4: `score-customers`
5. Phase 5: `detect-anomalies`
6. Phase 6: `generate-action-plan`
7. Phase 7: `produce-summary`
8. Phase 8: `submit`
9. Phase 9: `address-feedback`
10. Phase 10: `retrospective`
