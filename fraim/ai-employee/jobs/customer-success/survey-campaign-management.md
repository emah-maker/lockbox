# FRAIM Job: survey-campaign-management

## Intent
Launch NPS/CSAT surveys and targeted retention campaigns via the configured CRM, then analyze response clusters to surface actionable insight for the CS team.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/customer-success/survey-campaign-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing Survey/Campaign Details (type, status, audience), Response Count and Aggregate Scores, Cluster Analysis (top themes, trends, member counts), Delivery Stats, and Recommended Follow-Up Actions
- Survey or campaign created and launched (or scheduled) in the CRM
- For surveys with responses: feedback clusters and trend analysis
- Recommended follow-up actions based on cluster findings

2. Phase 2: `scope-intent`
3. Phase 3: `design-content`
4. Phase 4: `select-audience`
5. Phase 5: `launch`
6. Phase 6: `monitor-and-cluster`
7. Phase 7: `produce-summary`
8. Phase 8: `submit`
