# FRAIM Job: sprint-planning

## Intent
Turn a candidate backlog into a realistic sprint plan using WSJF-informed prioritization, explicit capacity limits, and a single sprint document the team can execute from.

## Outcome
- Final artifact: `docs/project-management/sprint-plan-{project}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`
- A normalized backlog with WSJF scores and rationale
- A committed sprint scope, stretch scope, and explicit not-in-sprint list
- Dependency, risk, and assumption notes that make tradeoffs auditable

2. Phase 2: `score-backlog`
3. Phase 3: `shape-sprint`
4. Phase 4: `produce-sprint-document`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
