# FRAIM Job: browser-application-validation

## Intent
To systematically validate an application in a browser, identify issues, and iterate until production-ready through comprehensive browser testing, ensuring a high-quality user experience.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/quality-assurance/browser-validation-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing executive summary, identified issues, validation results, and production readiness assessment with metrics and scorecards.
- **Identified Issues**: Documented issues categorized and prioritized.
- **Validated Application**: Confirmation of functionality across browsers and devices.
