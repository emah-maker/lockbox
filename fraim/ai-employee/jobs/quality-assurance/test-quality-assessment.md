# FRAIM Job: test-quality-assessment

## Intent
To perform a comprehensive evaluation of the test suite, assessing both its adherence to structural and naming standards and the depth of its functional coverage against defined validation plans. Use it when the question is "How strong is this project's test surface?"

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/quality-assurance/test-quality-report-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with standards compliance scorecard, coverage analysis dashboard, dimension scores (coverage, test integrity, test design, reliability), and prioritized gap remediation recommendations.
- **Standards Compliance**: Documentation of inheritance, naming, and utility usage results.
- **Coverage Analysis**: Identification of mission-critical scenarios lacking automated test evidence.
- **Actionable Remediation**: Prioritized recommendations for improving both test structure and coverage depth.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `test-standards-assessment`
2. Phase 2: `coverage-analysis`
3. Phase 3: `analyze-coverage-gaps`
4. Phase 4: `author-quality-report`
5. Phase 5: `quality-score-emission`
6. Phase 6: `test-quality-submission`
