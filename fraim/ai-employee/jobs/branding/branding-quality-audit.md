# FRAIM Job: branding-quality-audit

## Intent
Evaluate the strength and coherence of an existing brand, produce a stable quality score, and emit analytics-friendly quality metrics that can feed FRAIM dashboards and downstream improvement workflows.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/branding/audits/{slug}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing dimension scorecard (clarity, differentiation, coherence, proof, identity expressiveness, governance readiness), composite quality score, evidence highlights, and coaching recommendation
- Flat quality metrics emitted for analytics
- A coaching recommendation identifying the highest-leverage brand improvement

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `artifact-discovery`
2. Phase 2: `brand-surface-review`
3. Phase 3: `quality-scoring`
4. Phase 4: `coaching-recommendation`
5. Phase 5: `quality-score-emission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
