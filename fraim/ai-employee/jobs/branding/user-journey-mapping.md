# FRAIM Job: user-journey-mapping

## Intent
Map the end-to-end user journey — from first awareness through ongoing use — with emotion scoring at each touchpoint and identified design opportunities that reduce friction and improve key moments.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/design/{date}-user-journey-map-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx containing journey stages and touchpoints with user actions and evidence, emotion curve visualization with high and low points, and prioritized design opportunities ranked by impact and effort
- **Journey Map**: End-to-end journey with stages, touchpoints, user actions, and emotions at each step.
- **Emotion Curve**: Emotional high and low points identified and evidence-grounded.
- **Design Opportunities**: Prioritized opportunities ranked by improvement potential at high-pain touchpoints.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `discovery-interviews`
2. Phase 2: `touchpoint-mapping`
3. Phase 3: `emotion-scoring`
4. Phase 4: `opportunity-identification`
5. Phase 5: `journey-map-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
