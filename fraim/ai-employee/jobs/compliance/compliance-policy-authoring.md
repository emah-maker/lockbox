# FRAIM Job: compliance-policy-authoring

## Intent
Produce first-draft governance policies required by the active regulations — Information Security, Acceptable Use, Incident Response, Access Control, Data Retention & Disposal, Vendor Management, Change Management, Business Continuity, Data Classification — adapted from the template library under `templates/compliance/policies/` (each template fetched per policy via `get_fraim_file`).

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: Policy documents in .docx format at docs/compliance/policies/{policy-name}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with complete frontmatter (owner, last-reviewed, next-review, related-controls, applicable-regulations) and draft banner.
- **Draft banner**: Every generated policy carries "Requires legal review before adoption."
- **Gap closure**: `policy-completeness-check` subsequently passes for all required policies.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `policy-scoping`
2. Phase 2: `policy-drafting`
3. Phase 3: `policy-review`
4. Phase 4: `policy-submission`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
6. Phase 6: `retrospective`
