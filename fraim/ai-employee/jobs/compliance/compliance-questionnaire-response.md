# FRAIM Job: compliance-questionnaire-response

## Intent
Answer a customer security questionnaire (SIG Lite, CAIQ, or similar) OR review a customer-supplied DPA, drawing exclusively from the most recent `docs/compliance/compliance-review-*/` bundle. Unanswerable items are flagged, never fabricated.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/compliance/questionnaire-{customer}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx or docs/compliance/dpa-review-{customer}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with evidence-linked answers and unanswered items flagged.
- **Evidence-linked answers**: Every answer cites a specific evidence artifact in the latest bundle.
- **Unanswered items flagged**: Items with no supporting evidence carry `unanswered — no supporting evidence in latest compliance-review package`.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `questionnaire-ingest`
2. Phase 2: `evidence-matching`
3. Phase 3: `answer-drafting`
4. Phase 4: `self-review`
5. Phase 5: `response-submission`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
