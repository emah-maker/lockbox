# FRAIM Job: compliance-review

## Intent
Single-invocation compliance umbrella: confirm applicable regulations, build the cross-regulation control crosswalk, run per-regulation evidence-management jobs, aggregate gaps across regulations (dedup via crosswalk), check policy completeness, and bundle everything into an auditor-ready output at `docs/compliance/compliance-review-{YYYY-MM-DD}/`.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/compliance/compliance-review-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with executive summary, control crosswalk visualization, gap/remediation scorecard, policy status dashboard, and remediation priority matrix.
- Final artifact: a single compliance report at `docs/compliance/compliance-review-{YYYY-MM-DD}/README.md` containing the executive summary, control crosswalk, gaps and remediation plan, policy status, and run metadata as sections. The per-regulation evidence subfolders sit alongside it in the bundle directory; no other report files are created.
- **Deduplicated remediation issues**: One issue per unique control gap, labeled with every affected regulation.
- **Evidence feed**: The bundle is consumed by `generate-audit-evidence` and `soc2-evidence-management` as a first-class evidence source.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `applicable-regulation-confirmation`
2. Phase 2: `control-crosswalk-build`
3. Phase 3: `per-regulation-evidence-generation`
4. Phase 4: `gap-analysis-and-remediation-filing`
5. Phase 5: `policy-gap-check`
6. Phase 6: `compliance-report-bundle`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
