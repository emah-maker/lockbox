# FRAIM Job: soc2-evidence-management

## Intent
Generate comprehensive SOC2 compliance evidence packages by automatically collecting, documenting, and formatting evidence from project systems to demonstrate adherence to Trust Service Criteria during annual audits.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .pptx presentation at docs/compliance/soc2-evidence-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx with Trust Service Criteria scorecard, evidence collection status by control, remediation roadmap, and gap analysis with supporting evidence narratives.
- **Automated Collection**: Minimized manual effort in evidence gathering.
- **Gap Detection**: Identified missing controls and evidence with remediation plans.

3. Phase 3: `evidence-documentation-and-formatting`
4. Phase 4: `evidence-validation-and-gap-analysis`
5. Phase 5: `soc2-submission`

7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
