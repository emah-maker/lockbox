# FRAIM Job: issue-retrospective

## Intent
Capture learnings and insights from completed issues, enabling continuous improvement and preventing future similar problems through systematic analysis and documentation.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/retrospectives/issue-retrospective-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with Executive Summary, Timeline, Root Cause Analysis, Key Learnings, and Action Items
- **Root Cause Analysis**: Identified underlying causes, not just symptoms.
- **Actionable Prevention**: Specific measures to prevent recurrence.
- **Success Factors**: Documentation of what worked well.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `retrospective-creation`
2. Phase 2: `analysis-and-learning`
3. Phase 3: `prevention-measures`
4. Phase 4: `retro-submission`
5. Phase 5: `address-feedback`
