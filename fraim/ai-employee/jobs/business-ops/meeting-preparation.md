# FRAIM Job: meeting-preparation

## Intent
Prepare the manager for a specific meeting by assembling attendee context, prior-thread history, an agenda, and the decisions they must make, drawn from whatever connectors are configured.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/business-ops/meeting-prep-{slug}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with attendee context, context since the last meeting, a suggested agenda, decisions needed, and pre-reads.
- A prep brief specific to one meeting, source-tagged to the connectors that supplied each fact.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `identify-meeting`
2. Phase 2: `gather-connectors`
3. Phase 3: `assemble-brief`
4. Phase 4: `produce-prep`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
