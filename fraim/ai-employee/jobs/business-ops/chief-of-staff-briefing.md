# FRAIM Job: chief-of-staff-briefing

## Intent
Give the manager one daily briefing that answers "what needs me today?" by synthesizing across every connector they have configured (email, calendar, chat), rather than checking each tool separately.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/business-ops/chief-of-staff-briefing-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with the ranked priorities, the calendar strip, and a connector-availability summary.
- A ranked, source-tagged list of what needs the manager today, plus a calendar view annotated ATTEND / DELEGATE / SKIP.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `triage-scope`
2. Phase 2: `gather-connectors`
3. Phase 3: `synthesize-brief`
4. Phase 4: `produce-brief`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
