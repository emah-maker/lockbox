# FRAIM Job: analyze-transcript

## Intent
Turn a raw meeting or session transcript into structured, actionable insights — Items Learned, Actionable Items, and Other Important Notes — ready to act on or route into downstream FRAIM workflows.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: a .docx review copy generated from the final artifact via the author-docx skill at submission; this is the outcome delivered for review, with the .md below as the canonical agent-side record.
- Final artifact: `docs/business-ops/transcript-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`
- A markdown document with three structured sections approved by the user, grounded entirely in the transcript with no hallucinated owners, deadlines, or facts.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `intake-transcript`
2. Phase 2: `extract-insights`
3. Phase 3: `validate-output`
4. Phase 4: `present-for-approval`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
6. Phase 6: `address-feedback`
7. Phase 7: `retrospective`
