# FRAIM Job: operational-reporting

## Intent
Produce a reviewable operating, portfolio-impact, or stakeholder-status report from normalized evidence using one explicit report mode: `weekly-operating-review`, `portfolio-impact`, or `stakeholder-status`.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**:
  - `weekly-operating-review`: `docs/business-ops/operating-review-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx` or markdown brief with cash, pipeline, blockers, priorities, and required decisions.
  - `portfolio-impact`: `docs/business-ops/portfolio-impact-report-{program}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx` plus optional metrics CSV, evidence map, and founder-interview briefs.
  - `stakeholder-status`: `docs/delivery-ops/stakeholder-status-reporting-{project-name}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.pptx` with executive summary, RAG-scored workstream table, and actions/decisions.
- Evidence, assumptions, gaps, and report-mode constraints remain visible.
- The job creates the report or deck. External recipient-specific sending remains the job of `send-stakeholder-update`.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `load-reporting-context`
2. Phase 2: `select-report-mode`
3. Phase 3: `normalize-evidence`
4. Phase 4: `score-health`
5. Phase 5: `produce-report`
6. Phase 6: `submit`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
