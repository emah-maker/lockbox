# FRAIM Job: calendar-triage

## Intent
Defend the manager's calendar for the week by proposing, per meeting, whether to keep, delegate, decline, reschedule, or replace it with focus time, with every change gated on the manager's confirmation.

## Outcome
- **Reviewable deliverable**: A .docx document at docs/business-ops/calendar-triage-{YYYY-MM-DD}.docx with a per-event recommendation and a pending action for each proposed change.
- A triaged week where each meeting carries a recommendation, and each proposed change is a pending HITL action awaiting the manager's approval.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `triage-scope`
2. Phase 2: `gather-connectors`
3. Phase 3: `propose-changes`
4. Phase 4: `produce-triage`
5. Phase 5: `submit`
