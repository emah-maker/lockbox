# FRAIM Job: evolve-employee

## Intent
Help a manager evolve their AI employee in plain language without forcing them to choose up front between a job, skill, rule, template, script, or config change. The request can be seeded from a successfully completed run, an SOP or document, or a plain-language description.

## Outcome
- An approved employee-evolution brief covering:
  - what is not working today
  - what the employee should do differently
  - why the chosen intervention is the minimum effective change.
- New or updated artifacts at explicit target paths such as:
  - `fraim/personalized-employee/jobs/...`
  - `fraim/personalized-employee/skills/...`
  - `fraim/personalized-employee/rules/...`
  - `fraim/personalized-employee/templates/...`
  - `fraim/personalized-employee/scripts/...`
  - `fraim/config.json`
- A validation summary confirming:
  - the change matches the manager's plain-language intent
  - all edits stayed inside approved destinations
  - the resulting employee behavior is easier to trust and easier to manage.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `intake-request`
2. Phase 2: `diagnose-evolution`
3. Phase 3: `confirm-evolution-plan`
4. Phase 4: `apply-evolution`
5. Phase 5: `validate-evolution`
6. Phase 6: `submit`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
