# FRAIM Job: video-demo-to-sop

## Intent
Turn a video recording or transcript of a workflow demo into a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) that documents the workflow clearly enough for `evolve-employee` to generate a FRAIM job file from it.

## Outcome
- **New SOP file**: `docs/sops/<workflow-name>.docx` — a Word document capturing the workflow's trigger, steps, and expected outcomes, confirmed by the demo author.
- **evolve-employee invoked**: `evolve-employee` is called with the SOP docx path as input to generate the FRAIM job file.
- **Author-confirmed**: Intent and Outcome are confirmed by the demo author before the file is written.
- **Grounded**: every step in the SOP is traceable to something shown or narrated in the recording.
- **Next step identified**: the SOP is ready to pass to `evolve-employee` to generate a FRAIM job file.

## Steps
1. Phase 1: `intake-recording`
2. Phase 2: `correlate-content`
3. Phase 3: `infer-job-structure`
4. Phase 4: `gap-fill-review`
5. Phase 5: `write-sop`
6. Phase 6: `submit`
7. Phase 7: `address-feedback`
8. Phase 8: `retrospective`
